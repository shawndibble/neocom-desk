# Build Plan results show two verdicts and a gross/net profit toggle

Manufacturing a Build Plan's product can be evaluated two ways: is it cheaper
than buying it outright (an acquisition question — no market-selling fees
apply, since nothing is being sold), or is manufacturing-to-sell profitable
(a market question, net of sales tax and broker fee). These can disagree — a
product can be cheaper to build than buy while still losing ISK if resold —
so the Results panel shows both, separately labeled, rather than collapsing
them into one verdict. Profit/Margin/ISK-per-hour additionally toggle between
gross (before tax/broker) and net (after), because both are legitimate ways
industrialists reason about a job; Break-even Price stays a net figure always,
since it answers "at what price do I stop losing ISK," which only holds net
of the fees an actual sale pays.
