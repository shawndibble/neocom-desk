# Scope decisions — PI quick wins spend a colony's room once; storage saves are not added (issue #2769)

_Recorded 2026-10-06 · issue #2769. Code: `src/engine/pi/planAdvice.ts` (`spendRoomOnce`, `isSaving`, `totalQuickWins`), `src/features/pi/planAdviceModel.ts`._

- **A colony's spare and freed room is spent by one quick win.** Four kinds of win claim the same CPU and Powergrid: heads added in place of idle factories, more extractors, basic factories refining the colony's own raw, and new factories from the network plan. Only the claim that adds the most ISK a day stays. A claim with a figure beats one without, and a tie keeps the first. One source's factories on one host count as one claim, because that source already split the room between them. Before this, Uttindar V counted both "remove 3 idle factories and add 6 heads" and "1 more factory ... remove its idle factories first", and both went into the headline. Heads that lose fall back to "remove the idle factories", with no figure, so those factories are still named. Any other losing claim is dropped. Nothing re-prices a dropped win against the room the kept one leaves.
- **A storage win's figure is a saving, not an addition.** It is the income a full launchpad would lose before the pilot's haul. Plan shows it as "saves ~X/day" and Colonies as "Haul now: saves ~X/day". It is left out of every quick-win total: the Plan headline, the per-planet strip, after quick wins, and Colonies' small fixes. Its minutes still count. This supersedes bullet 3 of 20261005-123211 ("today plus every quick win is the colony running at full"). Today is still the nominal rate less the stalled share. A rebuild is now measured against today plus the quick wins that add, so a change's gain can include the stall a better-buffered layout no longer has.
