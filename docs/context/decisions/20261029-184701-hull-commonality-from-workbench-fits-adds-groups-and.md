# Scope decisions — Hull commonality from Workbench fits adds groups and breaks near-ties, never scores (issue #5581)

_Recorded 2026-10-29 · issue #5581._

- **Group level only.** Workbench fits give the overall idea of how a hull is flown, but their authors often do not know every variation, so commonality is the share of a job's fits carrying at least one module of a GROUP (SDE `groupID`), never of a type or variant; the engine's scores choose the variant. The one exception is the propulsion kind (afterburner, MWD, micro jump drive), a role choice #5578 needs: `commonPropulsionKind` and `JobCommonality.propulsion`. An afterburner or MWD is told apart by name (both are group 46); micro jump drives are group 1189.
- **Job from the fit name.** PvE, PvP and Explore keyword lists per the ticket (stems match a word's start; `l1`-`l5`, `rat`, `wh`, `pve`, `pvp`, `solo` match whole words). A name matching several jobs counts for each at full weight; a name matching none counts for every job at half weight. A job needs 10 matching fits, counted with those weights (an unnamed fit counts 0.5), else its commonality is null.
- **Only current fits count:** the Popular panel's out-of-date check (`checkWorkbenchFit`) decides, so a fit using a removed item is skipped.
- **Only adds, never restricts.** Groups on at least 25% of the job's fits join `reworkGroups` for the search (both the single swaps' Rework and the Makeover's moves and fills). In the single-swap candidates they sit in their own last tier with their own per-module budget, and in the Makeover they come after every move the table's groups make, so the 300-calculation and 400-candidate caps never drop a candidate the table finds. The fills' per-group share is still sized by the table's groups only. With no commonality passed, results are identical to before.
- **Tie-break, not a score term.** Gains within 1% of each other (relative) go to the target group more common on the hull for the job: in `findMakeover`'s pick (`targetShare`), in `rankImproveOptions`/`findImprovements`, and, so the first pick is not undone by a chain step, a later step that only swaps a module an earlier step placed for a less common group at a gain within 1% of the score so far is skipped. Variants of one group are never ordered by popularity, and a gain more than 1% ahead always wins.
- **Hints, shown not scored.** A Makeover row or single-swap row whose target group is on at least 50% of the job's fits shows "Ballistic Control Systems on 98% of PvE Drake fits" (the group is named by its market group, one level up for a size tier; the propulsion group has no honest single name, so no hint). A Makeover that takes a group off the fit that at least 80% of the job's fits keep shows a quiet caution line. Plain dim text, no control, so no new §6c cue.
- **Loading.** `useImprove` loads the hull's Workbench fits through `loadWorkbenchFits` (cached ten minutes, reduced once per cached list). A failed load, no fits or an unreadable game-data check is null and Improve behaves as before.
