# Scope decisions — The miner is trusted until the book closes the day; then the book's records stand

_Recorded 2026-11-25._

- **Until a Corp Tax Book closes a day, the miner's own numbers are the record.** The open day is owed at an estimate from what the miner listed for the corporation's own Payee (decision 20261125-171154), the same way a Payee that does not keep a book is owed. Nothing is checked against the book while the day is open.
- **Once the book closes the day, its records stand, and the miner's records follow them without asking.** Per the user: the miner is who we trust until then; after that we fix the miner's records to match the corporation's rather than show a difference to sort out. Ore the miner listed for the corporation's own Payee beyond what the book recorded is no longer a Conflict and is never Settling. The book's rows stand for the miner's whole entry for that Payee, and the excess is the miner's free ore again, labelled Unattributed like any ore the books left. If it was another moon's, the miner assigns it there. If the book later records it (a late refinery log), the book takes it from that free ore, so nothing is counted twice. This supersedes the over-listed Conflict (#6203) and the Settling window (decisions 20261124-211423 and 20261125-184957).
- **A quantity difference is only shown when the book bills more ore than the miner mined.** A book that recorded less is simply the bill; "Disputed: you X, book Y" and Compare with the book no longer appear for it. A book billing more than the miner's own log is still a difference the miner can raise, and rate differences are unchanged.
- **Out of scope here: ore the miner gave to another Payee that the book recorded** (`otherPayee`, `ownerCollected`, `ownNeedsReview` Conflicts). Those still resolve to the corporation after 14 days with Tell {corp} in between. Whether they also follow the book at once is a separate decision.
