# Scope decisions — Build profit and Trading profit become one Profit widget with a Source setting

_Recorded 2026-10-24._

- **One `profit` kind, titled "Profit", with a per-widget Source: Builds, Trading or Both.** Both widgets answered "what did I make over 7/30/90 days", so the Gallery offers one. Builds and Trading draw exactly what the two widgets drew; a new widget opens on Both. Supersedes the "own kind `tradingProfit`" point of decision `20261016-235250`; its pooling and broker-fee rules stand.
- **Both is the sum on Builds' periods.** Number: the combined total, with the vs-previous line when either readable source had a window before; the delta is combined now minus combined before, a source quiet then counting 0 (it really made nothing) and an unreadable Trading left out of both sides. Chart: builds and trades as two lines on the same day or week buckets, with Builds' axis labels (profit can be negative, which a stacked chart cannot show). Table: one row per product or item, tagged with its source, sorted by profit, toned like Trading's.
- **It moves to the Money Gallery group.** It no longer belongs to Industry or Trade alone; "what did I make" is a money question, beside Wallet and Net worth.
- **Known edge, no guard: a unit can count twice.** A built unit sold on the market is FIFO-priced against the earliest bought unit of the same type, so when the pilot also bought that type, the sale can show a trading margin while the build's own logged profit counts too. Separating built from bought stock needs per-unit provenance the wallet does not carry.
- **Only Trading alone sums across Characters.** Logged runs are read for the header Character, so Builds and Both read the header Character and narrow trades to it; All characters falls back with the usual note. Rules out a Both figure that pools one source and not the other.
- **Each Source loads and asks permission only for its own reads.** Builds reads no ESI and never asks for the wallet grant; Both keeps showing the builds with a Grant access under them when the header Character has no wallet read.
- **The retired kind migrates on read; absent Source means Builds.** A saved `tradingProfit` loads as Profit on Trading and a Profit saved before the setting as Builds, keeping size, view, scope and range, so nobody's widget changes. The layout syncs through the Firebase project production shares, and production's parse keeps unknown kinds and fields, so an older build reading a migrated layout draws Build profit and never drops the field.
