# Scope decisions — Corp deadline strip gets its own panel (issue #3451)

_Recorded 2026-10-10 · issue #3451._

- **The Deadline Strip is its own full-width Panel below Standing, with a severity glyph per day, and no month row or footnote.** Colour alone distinguished the bars (DESIGN.md §7), and sharing a row with the figures at `xl` squeezed the columns. The glyph replaces the legend swatch; the month row and footnote were redundant with the date labels and the accessible description.
