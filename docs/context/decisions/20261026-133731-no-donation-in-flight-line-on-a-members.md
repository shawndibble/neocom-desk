# Scope decisions — No donation-in-flight line on a member's corp balance

_Recorded 2026-10-26._

- **The member's corp balance no longer says "<amount> ISK sent, waiting for <Corp> to credit it."** Crediting a donation is the corp's side of the book; a member keeping their own books only needs the corp book when it disagrees with them (owes more, a conflict) or assigns their mining for them. The yellow line read as a problem when nothing was wrong. The donation matching itself (sender, amount, time) is unchanged, and the per-day "Sent, waiting" status still explains a row a member opens.
