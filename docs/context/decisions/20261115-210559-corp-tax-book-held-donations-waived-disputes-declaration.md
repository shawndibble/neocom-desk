# Scope decisions — Corp Tax Book held donations, waived disputes, declaration confirms and the stale-ledger mail claim

_Recorded 2026-11-15._

- **A donation held for review is matched once the ledger shows its Miner owed when they paid.** This amends `20261115-113253`, under which no later sync re-matched a held donation. The ESI ledger lags the mining by about an hour, so a Miner who pays straight after mining was held though they owed. A later sync (the officer visit or the scheduled run) now assigns a held donation (`reviewReason: 'no-open-balance'`) to its sender's Miner when the ledger holds a row for that Miner dated on or before the payment's UTC day and not yet billed when they paid (`unbilledWhenPaid`). Ore mined only after the payment never releases it, so a buyback is still not credited because the Miner mined again later. Assigning clears the mark, as an officer's assignment does.
- **The server prices a waived dispute from the Late notices that listed the day.** `dismissDispute` credits each disputed ore at the unit price and rate its notice lines billed, never more of it than they billed; the client's figure no longer sets the amount or decides the Director limit. A day no notice listed has no server-side price (prices and rates are resolved on the client), so its waiver counts as over the approval limit: a Director, or a book with no limit, may waive it at the client's figure; anyone else is refused. `dismissRecords` stays client-priced for now: most dismissed days are not billed yet, so it would need the same server-side rate and price resolution, which `functions/` does not mirror.
- **A declaration is resolved by the payment that confirms it, in the same write.** Recording a payment by hand or matching a wallet donation for a declaration carries its `declarationId`; the server writes the payment and the declaration's `confirmed` resolution (with the `paymentId`) in one transaction and refuses the second path with `already-resolved`. `resolveDeclaration` stays for rejecting, and for older clients.
- **A Miner may withdraw their own dispute until an officer answers it.** The dispute doc is deleted (audited as `withdrawDispute`), so it leaves Needs you and the day is billed as before; the Miner may flag the day again later.
- **A stale-ledger mail claim lapses after 4 hours unless marked sent.** The claiming officer's mail queue marks the spell sent (`staleMailSentFor`) when it sends a copy; an unsent claim older than 4 hours (`staleMailClaimedAt`) may be claimed by the next officer's client. Only a client that marks the mail sent claims with a time (`marksSent`); a claim from an older client, or stored before this, has none and stays final, so an older client's claim never lapses into a second mail.
