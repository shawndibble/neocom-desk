# Scope decisions — Out-of-date EVE Workbench fits are never listed

_Recorded 2026-10-04._

- **An out-of-date fit is never listed, priced or loadable from the EVE
  Workbench tab.** This replaces the "show out-of-date fits" toggle from
  `20261003-173240-out-of-date-eve-workbench-fits-removed-items.md`: a fit
  that won't work in today's game isn't worth a click. The tab still says so
  when every fit for a hull is out of date, rather than looking empty. The
  check itself (what counts as out of date) is unchanged.
