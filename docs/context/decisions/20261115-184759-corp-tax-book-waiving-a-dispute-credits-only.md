# Scope decisions — Corp Tax Book: waiving a dispute credits only the disputed ore

_Recorded 2026-11-15._ Amends `20261115-125600-corp-tax-book-a-miner-declares-and-disputes`.

- **An officer waives a Dispute's ore, not its day.** A Miner disputes some ore lines of a day, so "Dismiss the day" (a `dismissRecords` over the dispute's records at their full charge) waived ore nobody disputed. The new edit action `dismissDispute` writes one credit Adjustment on the day for the tax on the disputed lines only, priced by the client from the day charge's own prices and rate, and marks the dispute `resolution.kind: 'dismissed'` with that amount. The Director approval limit sees that amount. The Miner reads the credit on their day and "{corp} waived the tax on that ore". A dispute whose records a whole-day dismissal already covers still counts as answered. An Adjustment is not reversed the way a dismissal is; a mistaken waiver is corrected with another Adjustment.
- **The server checks what a Miner disputes against the record.** Each line's refinery, day and ore must be in the book's Observed Rows, and the quantity of an ore disputed across all its Payees and all calls no more than recorded. One day holds at most 100 lines however many calls built it. A dismissal amount of zero or none counts as over the Director limit, and `dismissDispute` refuses one outright. The server does not price ore itself: prices are the client's.
- **Confirming a declaration records its payment under `m_<declarationId>`.** A retry, or a second officer confirming, finds that payment already recorded and goes on to resolve the declaration, so the ISK is never recorded twice.
