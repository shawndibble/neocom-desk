# Scope decisions — Build Plan materials are grouped by errand

_Recorded 2026-10-03._

- **A Build Plan's Materials panel is a shopping list, in four sections.** Every row is filed under one errand (`materialErrands.ts`), first match wins: a Blueprint Acquisition row is **Blueprint**; a material being built here is **Building**; nothing left to get (fully owned, or a zero quantity) is **Already have**; everything else, including partly owned and unpriced rows, is **To buy**. Each section has a heading with its own colour, glyph, count and, for To buy and Blueprint, what it still costs, with unpriced rows counted beside the figure rather than priced at zero. Already have starts folded on every width and names its materials while folded. Sorting works within a section, never across them. The single flat table was the same rows with the errand left for the reader to work out from a glyph.
- **The reserved icon slot before every name is gone.** It existed so names lined up whether a row carried the build/buy toggle, the blueprint glyph or nothing, but on a phone card an empty slot read as an indent. Each errand's action is now a text link under the name: **Build instead** on anything something here can produce (green, with what it saves, only when the make-or-buy advice says building is cheaper; quiet otherwise), **Recipe · Buy instead** on a row being built, and **Change tier** on the blueprint, which opens the Blueprint Acquisition picker. Advice on a material nothing here can build keeps its marker, now inline after the name. Each link's accessible name begins with its visible text (WCAG 2.5.3).
- **Need, Have, To buy read left to right.** Owned is labelled **Have** in the panel and sits between Need and To buy, which is its own column instead of a "Need:" caption under the quantity. A Have or price the player typed is shown in the accent. On a phone the same subtraction is one line, with the Have field in it, so a phone row is name and total, then the subtraction, then the action and price, not a stack of six labels. A built row shows "—" for To buy, Price and Volume, not empty cells.
- **A row changes section only once focus leaves it.** A Have commit lands on blur, as the player tabs on; moving the row then would unmount the field focus was moving to. A section holds its rows in place while focus is inside it, and when they move, a toast with Undo confirms it. Only moves the player's own edits caused get the toast: "Use all", or a sub-build changing how much of an input is needed, moves rows without one. Switching a row between buying and building moves it straight away, with its own Undo, and focus follows it to its new section.
- **Volume is dropped only beside Costs & revenue.** From `xl` to `2xl` the panel shares its row with Costs & revenue, which already totals the plan's volume, and the table has to fit without a sideways scroll (#2165). Every other width keeps the column.
