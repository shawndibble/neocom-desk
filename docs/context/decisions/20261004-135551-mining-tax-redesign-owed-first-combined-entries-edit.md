# Scope decisions — mining tax redesign: owed first, combined entries edit as one

_Recorded 2026-10-04. Direct request from the pilot, from a UX review with mockups (direction A, "Open + History", plus flows F1–F6 and the desktop layout). Their usage: one active miner, rarely more than three Payees owed at once, long Payee names, sessions that run past midnight UTC (7 pm Central), rare splits, settling up at the end rather than per entry._

- **Owed first, history last.** The Tax tab opens on who the pilot owes (one compact card per owed Payee: name on its own line, amount, Settle up and Link payment), then **Open** (unassigned, needs review, outstanding), then **History** (paid, dismissed) grouped by EVE month, every month folded. History is low priority for this pilot, so it never sits between them and a bill. Retires the Status filter dropdown and its remembered `miningTaxStatusFilter` preference, and the "Settled payees" toggle: settled Payees are one line of names under the owed cards. `DataTable`'s `groupBy` gained `minSize` so a one-entry month still folds under its own header.

- **One attention strip.** Re-login, unpriced ore, sell-price fallback, duplicate Assignments and unclassified ore render as rows of one collapsible strip instead of five stacked banners. Collapsed by default; the header still counts them.

- **Room for long Payee names.** The Character column shows only when the rows in view come from more than one pilot. The Payee column's cap widens at `lg` and again from 1200px; the Ore column (type icons) only shows from 1200px, where it fits beside a full name without pushing Status off-screen at 1024px (issue #2147). Phone rows use the dense two-line card with tax owed as the headline figure.

- **A combined entry edits as one** (supersedes the per-member editing in `20260906-091857` and the "primary member only" note in `20260927-105434`). The combined view keeps each EVE day's own ore and value → tax line but has a single Edit, which opens one form: Payee and tax % once for every day, then each day's per-ore values. `updateCombinedAssignments` writes every member in one `bulkPut` and keeps the `groupId`, so a Payee or rate change moves the whole entry (unlike `updateAssignment`, which ejects a member whose terms change — that rule still holds for the single-entry editor). A day nobody touches keeps the value and tax it was billed at; an edited day is re-totalled from its lines and stores them as `oreLineValues`. Each line starts at its share of the stored value (by market worth on the mined date, or by units when unpriced), so the boxes add up to the billed figure. Unlocking a Paid combined entry only opens the fields — nothing is written until Save, and Save corrects the figures without un-paying the entry.

- **A combined entry's own actions.** Settle up while a day is owed, Accept new total when a paid day grew (every grown day at once — the combined view is the only place a member is reachable now), and a More menu with Link a wallet payment, Take a day out, Uncombine all and Unassign all days (asked first).

- **Take a day out / Uncombine.** `uncombineAssignments` clears `groupId` and nothing else, so a day leaves a combined entry with its Assignment, figures and payment intact. Fixes the "split one apart first" dead end (Split is still not offered on combined members).

- **Unpaid combined entries absorb new ore** (supersedes `20260926-132951`'s "grouped growth still flags"). The pilot combines while still mining the second day; flagging that growth as Needs Review contradicted the "unpaid growth has no history to protect" rule. Paid and dismissed members still flag.

- **Continue a session across midnight UTC.** A wholly unassigned entry whose pilot mined the same system the EVE day before, where that day has exactly one Assignment and it is still owed, is offered as "Continue the <date> session" (same Payee and tax %, combined via `joinAssignments`). Asked by default — one system can hold several Payees' moons — with an opt-in device-local automatic mode that shows Undo, and "Keep separate" remembered per entry (pruned to entries still in the ledger). Ticking "Do this automatically next time" leaves the offers already on screen as offers; the header's ⋯ menu carries the same switch, so automatic mode can be turned off again. A dismissed slice of the previous day doesn't block the offer. Neither preference is a view preference.

- **Suggest the Payee last used in the system** (supersedes the single remembered `systemId` and the AssignDialog "Remember this system" checkbox from `20260905-180422`). `suggestPayeeForSystem` pre-selects the Payee of the pilot's most recently mined Assignment in that system and orders the rest by how often they were used there; the legacy `systemId` is only a fallback when the system has no history. With no history at all, still nothing is pre-selected — the "never pick a Payee the pilot didn't choose" rule from `20260905-170644` stands.

- **Link a wallet payment to owed entries by hand.** A new picker (from an owed card, the entry's More menu, or Settle up's "Already in my wallet? Pick it") lists unlinked Made Payments from the pilots who mined those entries, pre-ticks the oldest entries the amount fully pays, and marks them paid with one shared payment that links the transfer — learning the Payee's `entityId` when confirmed. The selection bar offers it too when the ticked owed entries share one Payee, and Settle up hands over the entries it was settling rather than the whole balance. Fills the gap where the manual picker only existed for Paid rows and the "Payments to link" card only offered plausible matches.

- **Settle up is one screen.** Entries (folded to a summary line for a single entry), copyable amount/recipient/reason (one amount and recipient per Payee when a selection spans several), date and method, then record. "Sent a different amount?" pays oldest entries first, only ones it covers in full (`allocateOldestFirst`); the rest stay owed — there is still no partly paid Assignment. A contract id is now recorded as a manual `contractLinks` entry rather than the legacy `contractId` field.

- **Entry detail leads with one action.** An unassigned entry opens straight into the Assign form; an assigned one opens as a summary with one primary action for its status (Settle up its Payee when owed, Accept new total when grown) and Edit. Split, Combine, Link a wallet payment, Link a transaction and Unassign live in a More menu, and Unassign asks first.

- **Payees as two-line rows.** Name on line one with the owed balance — Outstanding entries only, the same figure as the owed cards — (or Settled); rate, hub and the systems mined for them on line two. The add form hides behind "Add Payee". Deleting a Payee that is still owed offers to move its owed entries to another Payee (`moveAssignmentsToPayee`) or delete anyway, which leaves them as "Unknown Payee" as before.

- **"Combine" is the word.** The UI said both "Join" and "Combine"; it now says Combine everywhere (the i18n keys keep their `join*` names). Added **Combined Entry** to `CONTEXT.md`.

- **Out of scope here:** the "Mining now" card (direction B, not chosen), split by share, bulk "Assign N to…", account-wide Payees.
