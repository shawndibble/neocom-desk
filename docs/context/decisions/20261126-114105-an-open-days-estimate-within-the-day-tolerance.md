# Scope decisions — An open day's estimate within the day tolerance reads Paid

_Recorded 2026-11-26._

- **Credit that leaves an open day's estimate short by no more than the book's day tolerance pays it in full.** The estimate reads Paid and nothing is left Outstanding. A member who paid in full and mined no more was shown a few thousand ISK owed because the live price drifted after the payment. The close-out already forgives that much when the day closes (20261125-171154), so showing it earlier only adds noise. It uses the book's own day tolerance, 0.5% unless an officer changed it. A larger gap, such as more ore mined after the payment, still stays Outstanding as its own row. This narrows the price-move clause of 20261125-171154's "Paying early pays the estimate from credit".
