# Scope decisions — Below market values copies at a resale floor and the widget reads the last view (issue #3989)

_Recorded 2026-10-16 · issue #3989._

- **A line with no market is valued at the cheapest _other_ single-type public offer of the same thing, only with at least 3 such offers, else 0.** A copy matches blueprint and exact ME/TE and prices at ISK/run × runs; anything else matches type and prices per unit. Comparables are fixed-price contracts that sell only that thing: no auctions, PLEX asks, item asks or barters, and a contract that also sells the original does not count. The contract being valued is never its own comparable. This differs from Assets' median rule (decision 20260930-120122) on purpose: an asset estimate wants a typical price, but a pilot reselling what they just bought must undercut the cheapest seller, so the floor is the honest figure here. Assets keeps the median.
- **A copy is never valued at its blueprint type's market price.** That price is the original's; a copy has no market of its own.
- **Market lines pay sales tax; contract-priced lines do not.** Selling into buy orders charges tax; reselling on a contract does not.
- **The Value cell names the weakest evidence.** A contract with any contract-priced line reads "from N contracts", N being the smallest comparable count behind any such line; otherwise "market".
- **Bar filters apply at contract grain.** A contract passes when any of its lines passes, so a search names a contract by one of its items. Below market always reads every type, whatever the search: a contract's other lines and the comparables are of other types.
- **The Overview widget reads the last Below market view, never the snapshot.** Item search saves the count and the top 10 contracts to the `belowMarketLastView` device setting when the view lands; the widget adds jumps from the header Character's Current System. Valuing the snapshot downloads every contract and prices thousands of types at the hub, which the Overview must not start on every load — the Hauling widget's rule (#3579). The age shown is the snapshot's own; refresh by opening Below market.
