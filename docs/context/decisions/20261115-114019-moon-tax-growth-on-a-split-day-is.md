# Scope decisions — Moon tax: growth on a split day is reviewed, and every split offers the Growth Collector (issue #6205)

_Recorded 2026-11-15 · issue #6205._

- **Growth on a split day is flagged Needs review, never absorbed.** A day with two or more Assignments (paid or not) is a split day. ESI's ledger lags up to an hour (`oreArrival.ts`), so ore reported after a split may be either moon's, and only the pilot knows. The unpaid Growth Collector flips to `needs-review` with the before/after diff instead of growing silently. A sole Assignment, including a member of a combined entry, still absorbs unpaid growth (decision 20260926-132951, 20261004). This narrows 20260926-132951's "unpaid growth is absorbed" to sole Assignments. It also covers the unpaid entry made from a paid day's late ore: that day has two Assignments, so its growth is reviewed too. Paid stays paid (ADR 0024) is unchanged: a paid Assignment never grows, and its late ore stays unassigned.
- **Every split offers the Growth Collector choice.** A second split (a third Assignment already collecting) used to hide the choice, so the new side could never collect. Now the dialog offers the side that collects now (the default there), the kept side, or the new side; picking a side moves the flag off the third Assignment in the same write, so an entry keeps one Growth Collector. The first split still defaults to the new side (20261114-211922): with growth now reviewed, a wrong default costs a review, not a wrong bill. Keeping more than ESI reports is allowed on a second split too; it hands later ore to the new side.
- **A Payee named for a Corp Tax Book corporation is offered the link.** The book matches a Payee by corporation id only, so an unlinked "Corp A" is a second, conflicting Payee. Manage Payees shows "Link to Corp A" when the typed name matches (whole name, ignoring case and outer spaces) a corporation whose Corp Tax Book the member can see. It is a suggestion, not automatic: a moon or place label may share a name. Other corporation names are still covered by the existing exact-name lookup on save, which links a single ESI match.
- **The Unattributed hint tells the miner what to do.** "No Corp Tax Book claims this ore. Assign it to your Payee if you owe someone." The old copy said nobody was owed, which is wrong for ore owed to a corporation with no book.
