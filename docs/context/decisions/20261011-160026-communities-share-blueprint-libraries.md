# Scope decisions — Communities share blueprint libraries

_Recorded 2026-10-11._

- **A Community's Member is a Character, never a person.** EVE exposes no account identity (see **Account**), so a player's alts each join, and each appears, separately. The share picker lists every Character on the device and offers a "join with another Character" shortcut. Person-level identity would mean building the Account concept the glossary rejects.
- **Sharing is opt-in per Community, per blueprint.** Nothing is shared after joining; the Member picks on the join page with share all, share none, search, a market-group tree and a live count. A category or single blueprint can be left out. An alt without `esi-characters.read_blueprints` is listed as "needs permission" and runs the normal grant flow.
- **Shared data is refreshed automatically, with writes kept low.** The device pushes only when its blueprint list's hash differs from the last push, and stamps each Member's library with "as of". After 30 days a Member reads as stale: greyed and badged, never hidden. Logging a Character out of the device asks whether to remove its shared blueprints too, defaulting to yes.
- **Location is the station or structure plus its system.** Container and hangar detail stay private. Only Members of that Community can read it.
- **One admin, transferable.** The admin can remove a Member, revoke or regenerate the invite link, and delete the Community. Leaving or being removed deletes that Member's shared data in the same step, never a soft hide. Co-admins are out for v1.
- **Invite link only in v1.** Multi-use, no expiry, revocable, with a preview page (name, admin, member count) before sign-in, and a cap of 100 Members. There is no public directory. In-game mail invites, sharing fittings (reusing the **Fitting Share Code**) and corp-owned blueprints are later tickets.
- **A Character joins at most 10 Communities.** Community Offers from all of them merge into BPC Sourcing, one row per sharer however many Communities show it, with a Community chip and a Community filter.
- **Community Offers feed Blueprint Acquisition at 0 ISK.** They join the tier pool, so the Acquisition Verdict can flip to "build". An owned BPO still wins over a Community Offer, and a Community Offer wins over any priced contract offer. A Member's reaction formula is never a source: formulas cannot be copied. A Shared BPO reads "ask <name> to copy": free to the buyer, but the owner pays the copy job.
- **The Community Library lists every blueprint.** Market, contract-only and LP-store blueprints all count, so BPC Sourcing and the Library cover the same set; the Library's extra is that Members are another source. Default view is "owned by nobody", grouped by market group with a count per category. A wide blueprint × Member matrix on desktop collapses to a list with an avatar stack of who holds each one on a phone.
