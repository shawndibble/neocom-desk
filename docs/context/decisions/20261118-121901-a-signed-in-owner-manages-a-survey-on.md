# Scope decisions — A survey's owner and its starting device manage it on its public page, and the details panel folds

_Recorded 2026-11-18._

- **On `/s/<id>`, a pilot signed in with the Character that started the survey gets what the Survey tab gives its owner: the editable Additional information panel and the scan list that sets a scan aside.** The owner opens the link as often as the tab (it is what they paste into the game), so making them go to the app to fix a bad paste or add a location was a detour. Ownership is the same name check the tab uses (`ownsSurvey`), a hint that only picks which controls to show. A visitor, or a pilot who is not the owner, sees the page as before.
- **The device that started the survey (`startedHere.ts`) gets the same, signed in or not.** It already edited the details with no account; it now also sets scans aside, and keeps the editor after signing in while the survey still names no owner (it was started with no account, so there is no name for `ownsSurvey` to match). Shawn chose this over requiring a sign-in for removals.
- **`firestore.rules` now lets anyone create a `surveyIgnores` doc, not only a signed-in pilot,** as `surveyTax` and `surveyInfo` already do. The rules never could tell the owner, so this was always a UI guard; the worst a stranger with the link can do is hide a scan through the API, and the owner or starting device restores it (newer doc, never a delete). Not deployed by CI: the rules need deploying before a signed-out device's removal lands.
- **The Additional information panel folds for the pilot who edits it, and starts folded on a survey this device just started from `/survey` with no details.** There is nothing to read in an empty panel, and it pushes the chart and scans down. Any detail stored, or a survey this device did not start, opens it. Applying a template opens it, so the rows are there to settle. Readers' read-only panel does not fold.
- **`/survey` has its own tab title** ("Start a mining survey"), instead of falling through to "Page not found". The page always rendered; only the title was wrong.
