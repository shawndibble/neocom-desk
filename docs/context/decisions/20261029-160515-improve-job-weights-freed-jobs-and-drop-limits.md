# Scope decisions — Improve Job weights, freed jobs and drop limits (issue #5564)

_Recorded 2026-10-29 · issue #5564._

- **Three jobs, each its own table entry in `improveJob.ts`.** PvE, PvP, Explore (Explore has a space, Dangerous by default). Tuning a job is a data change; an absent job behaves exactly as before.
- **Freed jobs.** PvE frees `scram` and `web` (the search may drop the last scrambler or web); PvP and Explore free none.
- **Best overall weights (multipliers on the `gainMetrics` terms, sustained tank replacing active repair).** PvE: dps 1, ehp 1, sustained 1, speed 0.25, align 0.25, lock range 0.25, reach (optimal, falloff, tracking) 0.5, capacitor 0.25. PvP: dps 1, ehp 1, sustained 0.25, speed 1, align 0.75, lock range 0.5, reach 0.5, capacitor 0.25. Explore: dps 0.25, ehp 0.5, sustained 0.5, speed 0.75, align 1, lock range 0.25, capacitor 0.25, reach 0.25 (the ticket gave no Explore reach value; an explorer rarely shoots). A job's weights replace the old armed/unarmed `OVERALL_WEIGHTS` rule, and the capacitor turning stable or unstable still counts whole. Role stats count whole.
- **Drop limits, as a fraction of the open fit's own figure, checked on every Makeover step and the final fit.** PvE: damage 10%. PvP: damage, speed, EHP 10%. Explore: speed 10%; Dangerous adds EHP and sustained tank 10%. A limit on the goal's own stat (damage for More damage, EHP for Tankier, speed for Faster) never binds. A breach of damage or speed reads as `keepsStats`, of EHP or sustained as `keepsTank`.
- **Single swaps are guarded only by Explore Dangerous's tank limits,** the `keepsTank` Guardrail generalised from conversions to every swap. Safe leaves tank modules free to swap out, and the loss still shows under Trade-offs.
- **Explore also holds the explorer's kit and, in Dangerous space, its tank modules, at the candidate level.** The real-engine Astero showed limits alone are not enough: a Makeover swapped one of two Relic/Data Analyzers for a scan array (the last-of-a-job guard allows it while another copy remains), and dropped Small Armor Repairer II because the sustained figure does not see a repairer the capacitor cannot sustain with the MWD running. So under Explore every copy of cloak, analyzer, probe launcher and propulsion stays (a swap within its group is fine), and in Dangerous a tank module group only changes to another tank group. This is what makes the ticket's acceptance (Dangerous keeps Small Armor Repairer II and Damage Control II; both analyzers stay) hold.
- **Not done here:** default Damage and Target Profiles per job, UI and persistence (#5567).
