# Scope decisions — Fittings Load launches drones up to what the pilot can field

_Recorded 2026-09-25._

- **A Loaded Fitting launches its drones once its first stats are in, as far as the hull's bandwidth and the pilot's drone count allow.** A pasted drone boat used to open with every drone in the bay and read 0 drone DPS until the drones were launched by hand — the loaders' "carried, not deployed" reading of EFT (`eftLoader.ts`) was right about the text but wrong about what someone pasting a fit wants to see. The loaders still produce bay stacks; the workspace then launches them in the order the fit lists them, limited by each drone's own bandwidth, the hull's total, and the pilot's Max Active Drones (the Drones skill: none without it, five at V), all read from the engine rather than assumed.
- **Only Loads whose source says nothing about which drones are out:** EFT, DNA and chat links, EVE XML, In-game Fittings and killmails. A Share Link and a My Fittings record carry explicit in-space and in-bay counts and open exactly as saved.
- **The launch is part of the Load, not an edit of its own.** It writes over the Load's history entry (no extra Back step to an all-in-bay copy), happens once per Load, and any edit made before the stats arrive cancels it. The Load itself never waits for the engine download.
- **The editor only, for now.** Fitting Compare's own Load (its picker) still opens drones in the bay, as before; the launch rule lives in the editor's workspace, and Compare would need its own stats pass per slot to apply it.
- **Saving, switching Character, or any edit before the stats arrive cancels the launch** — what is saved is what is on screen, and a launch decided on another Character's drone count would be wrong. A drone the engine gives no bandwidth for stays in the bay. A Load too large to link launches locally without touching the URL, since it never wrote one.
