# Scope decisions — Chunk ore from the later moon notifications and moon ore variants split by what was mined

_Recorded 2026-11-22._

> Amends `20261121-112002-moon-chunk-ore-make-up-source-order-and.md`: "the ExtractionStarted notification first" now reads "a moon drill notification first".

- **A chunk's ore comes from any moon drill notification that names it, the started one first.** Extraction finished, Laser fired and Automatic fracture carry the same `oreVolumeByType` as Extraction started. The started one is weeks old when the chunk pops and is often gone from ESI's feed, so a chunk fell back to a survey or a chunk-rate guess while the exact figure sat in the holder's notifications. The started notification's ore, once read or stored, is never replaced by a later one. A later notification also attaches its ore to a stored Extraction ESI no longer lists (matched by structure, moon and window), keeping the stored start.
- **A notification names only base moon ores; each base ore is split into the variants that make it up.** EVE decides which rocks come up as a variant (+15%/+100%, e.g. Brimful/Glistening Bitumens) when the chunk fractures, and the ledger records each variant as its own type. Compared type by type, every variant mined matched nothing: a moon mined to 80% read 50%, with a false loss on the base ore. The table is `MOON_ORE_VARIANTS` (`src/engine/corpTaxBook/moonOreVariants.ts`, the SDE's, pinned by a test against `public/data/types.json`; mirrored in `functions/src/corpTaxBookEngine.ts`).
- **The split is what was mined of that ore, else a linked survey's; mined always wins.** A survey scanned part-way through mining is skewed, so it stands in only until the ledger shows any of that ore. Mined wins also over a make-up the survey already split (a survey-sourced chunk, or a page that applied the survey before its ledger rows were read, like the Refinery page from moon docs): each base ore's family is re-split by what was mined. With neither, the base ore stays whole. Each family's total is kept exactly. Mined %, the ore-loss panel and the estimated value all read the split; a variant's loss is its share of its base ore's (residue is taken as falling evenly across them). A page that cannot price a variant keeps the value it had.
- **A linked survey's first scan is kept on the Extraction (`surveyScan`).** A Survey is a 7-day share link, and the book read it live, so a chunk sized by a survey lost its make-up a week later (its ore turned "From what was mined" and its loss Unknown). The link dialog now sends the first counted scan's per-ore volume, whole volume and time with `linkSurvey`; the page reads the live Survey while it exists and the kept scan after. Surveys keep expiring as before. A survey linked before this keeps no scan until it is linked again.
- **The split is derived, not stored.** It is worked out at view time, like the rest of the make-up; the server's moon docs count a variant mined against its base ore the same way. Stored moon docs are not rebuilt for it: the client recomputes mined % from each doc's mined ore, and a doc's own `minedPct` catches up when its Extraction or rows next change.
