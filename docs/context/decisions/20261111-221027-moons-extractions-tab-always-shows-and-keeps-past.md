# Scope decisions — Moons Extractions tab always shows and keeps past extractions

_Recorded 2026-11-11._

- **The Extractions tab (and the Overview moons panel) always show for anyone who can read the book.** This reverses the rule that hid them for a book with no refinery of its own (#4513). An accounting corporation's CEO who added their other corporations as moon owners expects the tab by default, whatever data is behind it. It rules out any "has own refineries" visibility check.
- **Every Extraction state, Expired included, is on by default in the Extractions tab.** Going back should show past pops beside the coming ones; a chip still narrows it.
- **The Moons subtabs are listed in the rail only when the page shows a tab bar.** A character who lands on the no-access page (a moon lender with no officer role) gets the one Moons entry and no subtabs.
