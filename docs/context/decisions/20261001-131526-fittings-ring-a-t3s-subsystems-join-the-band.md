# Scope decisions — fittings ring: a T3's subsystems join the band

_Recorded 2026-10-01._

- **A T3's subsystems sit on the Ring's band, upper-left between the rigs and the highs, not in a row beneath it.** Supersedes the "subsystems sit below the ring" bullets of `20260924-184905` and `20260924-205720`. Clockwise the band reads rigs, subsystems, highs, mids, lows, the highs still centred over the top; on a bare hull with no highs yet, the subsystems centre on the left.
- **To make room, a T3's band draws only its own slots — no outlines of the positions it lacks — and its racks close to one empty position apart.** Eight highs, mids and lows plus rigs and subsystems can't all go round one band, and a T3's rack sizes come from its subsystems anyway. Any room to spare collects across the bottom, between the lows and the rigs, rather than spreading the racks; if a fit's slots don't fit, the gaps close to the tactical ring's spacing first, then the pitch. Every other hull's ring is unchanged.
- **The calibration band therefore sits beside the subsystems on a T3, not the rigs.** The rim gauges keep their fixed positions on every hull.
