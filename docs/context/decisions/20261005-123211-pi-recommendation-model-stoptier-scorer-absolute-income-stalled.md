# Scope decisions — PI recommendation model: stopTier scorer, absolute income, stalled storage (issue #2632)

_Recorded 2026-10-05 · issue #2632. Code: `src/engine/pi/planAdvice.ts`, `planHaul.ts`, `planRecipes.ts`, `src/features/pi/planAdviceModel.ts`._

- **A rebuild is scored by `recommendStopTier`, not `planBest`.** `planBest` hosts a multi-planet goal chain. The Plan tab asks a different question, "what is the best one planet can make from its own ground", which is `recommendStopTier`'s. Its candidates are filtered to P1 and one-planet P2 and re-picked by the engine under the pilot's preference (Most ISK, or Least hauling). Raw P0 and P3+ are never offered.
- **Every figure is absolute ISK a day, never a margin over raw.** `ScoredStopTier.marginPerHour` for a made tier is net of the raw P0 it consumes, while Today (`builtColonyEarnings`) is an absolute income. The model rebuilds each candidate's income from the scorer's own unit rate (units x bid after sales tax, less customs on the one export), so a rebuild is compared with today absolute against absolute.
- **A colony whose storage fills before the pilot's haul does not earn its nominal rate.** Today is the nominal figure less the stalled share, and the storage quick win gives that share back. Today plus every quick win is therefore the colony running at full, with nothing counted twice. A colony whose extractors have all stopped earns 0 (a measurement), not "unknown".
- **A rebuild is measured against today after quick wins.** The pilot does the quick wins first, so the rebuild's gain is what is left on top. Totals are today, after quick wins and after rebuild, each summed over colonies with a figure and counting the ones left out.
- **A recipe that needs a higher Command Center is offered only within the pilot's trained Command Center Upgrades skill,** scored at the lowest level that hosts it, and shown with an "upgrade first" step. An untrained or unknown skill offers none.
- **Least hauling picks the lowest m3 a day among recipes that still earn at least half the best recipe's ISK.** Without the floor it would pick a recipe that earns almost nothing because it also moves almost nothing. A rebuild is offered under it only when it hauls at least 5% less than the colony does today.
- **Corp buyback pays a share of the hub sale price with no sales tax,** and buying stays at the hub's ask. Customs is paid at the colony's office either way.
- **The recipe ranking assumes what the pilot has not told us, and says so.** It scores a hypothetical planet of each type at the pilot's own mean measured extraction rate (else the typed fallback), their trained Command Center level (else IV, flagged), a link cost borrowed from their colonies (else a stated ~50 km hop, flagged) and the storage their colonies typically carry. `rankingBasis` reports which were assumed, so the page can badge the figures as estimates.
- **"Room for factories" reuses the network plan's opportunities,** the Advisor's own detector, and needs two measurable colonies. It only offers a P2 no single colony already makes both inputs for, so a win usually draws on another colony's surplus and names it. Spare room for extractors is priced only for a colony on a single resource, since with two no figure says which one a new extractor would pull.
