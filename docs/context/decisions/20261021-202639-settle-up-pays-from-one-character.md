# Scope decisions — Settle up pays from one Character

_Recorded 2026-10-21 · player feedback: two Characters' entries for one Payee settled as one figure, and Open in EVE opened in the wrong Character's client._

- **Settle up has a payer: "Paying from".** ISK leaves one Character's wallet, and Payees are shared across Characters, so one balance can span several. When the offered entries do, Settle up shows a "Paying from" picker (a segmented row up to four Characters, a list beyond), each with its subtotal. Only the payer's entries start ticked; the others wait collapsed under "Also pay for other characters", so paying an alt's tax from a main is still one tick away. Rules out the old single blended figure no single transfer pays.
- **Default payer.** From one entry (row or combined row detail): the Character who mined it, and only that entry ticked; the same Character's other entries for that Payee wait unticked under "Also pay N other entries". From a balance card or the checkbox selection: the one Character if every entry is theirs; else the active Character when it has some; else the Character owing most (`settlePayer.ts`). Rules out a row's Settle up paying the whole Payee balance.
- **Open in EVE sends from the payer, on its own row.** It reads "Open in EVE · <payer>" under the To row (too long to share it). If the payer lacks the Open in EVE Permission it stays, `aria-disabled`, with "<payer> hasn't granted Open in EVE" and a grant link that makes the payer active first (Permissions shows the active Character); it never sends from another Character. It still hides when no tracked Character holds the Permission (DESIGN §6c).
- **Mining Tax remembers its Character filter.** The Tax tab joins the per-page picks of `20261011-082136` (page `miningTax`), but its first visit stays on All characters rather than the Settings default: the balances are meant to show every Character's debts until the pilot narrows them. Rules out defaulting Tax to This character, which the feedback suggested; the payer, not the filter, keeps transfers apart.
