# Scope decisions — Money taken off a Miner releases their recorded paid-through

_Recorded 2026-11-21._

- **An officer's act that takes money off a Miner drops that Miner's recorded paid-through day.** This covers moving a payment to another Miner, taking it off to Unmatched (`reassignPayment`), and marking more of it "not tax" (`markNotTax`), which is also how a hand record is undone. The book drops `meta/paid.minerPaidThrough.<minerId>` and the Miner's `minerPaid` copy. The next officer load works the account out without that money and records a new day, only over days still paid at saved prices. Before, the day stayed: the days the money had paid stayed closed, their shortfall was written off, and the Miner's Owed stayed 0.
- **Why the marker is dropped, not moved back to a day.** The server holds no record of which days a payment paid. Money pays the oldest unpaid day whenever it arrives, and credit pays days mined after it, so the payment's own date bounds nothing. The recorded account lists only the days still owed, never the ones paid. Any day the server picked would be a guess, and a wrong one keeps days closed for good. The officer's client is what set the day, so it sets it again.
- **Supersedes, for these acts only,** "a recorded-paid day whose money was later taken back stays settled" (20261119-170645) and "even when a payment is later marked not tax" (20261117-093535). Everything else still never reopens a recorded-paid day (D22): late ore, price changes, an exemption, a dispute.
- **The money's new Miner keeps its marker.** Money added never reopens a day. Neither Miner's snapshot changes: money dated before the lock date cannot be moved at all (decision 20261120-235011, "a lock date"), and a snapshot covers only days before it. The paid-through day is dropped, never rolled back to an earlier one: no history of it is kept.
