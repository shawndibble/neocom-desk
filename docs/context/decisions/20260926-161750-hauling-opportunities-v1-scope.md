# Scope decisions — Hauling Opportunities v1 scope

_Recorded 2026-09-26._

- **Hauling Opportunities is a tab of the Market page (`/market/hauling`), not a new route.** It prices at Trade Hubs like Appraisal does and reads the same market data, so it lives beside it. It has its own From/To hub pickers and does not use the page header's hub picker.
- **The price shown is an Expected Sell Price: one a hauler would list at to sell, never above today's cheapest listing.** A tool that prints destination-lowest-sell minus origin-lowest-sell gives every user the same list, and the destination's cheapest listings fill with copies of the same haul before the cargo lands. The estimate is the lower of one tick under the cheapest listing and the volume-weighted _median_ price the item sold at over the last 7 days (30 if nothing traded in that window). The median is deliberate: one stray trade at a wild price moved a mean anywhere on real thin markets. An earlier version used the price a week of sales reaches up the ladder, which could sit above the cheapest listing and so assumed waiting out every cheaper seller while the Days to Sell beside it charged for exactly that; the two figures contradicted each other, so it was dropped.
- **Units within 1% of the expected price count as ahead of the hauler.** Those sellers relist a tick below a new order, which is the undercutting the original complaint about price-gap tools describes. Days to Sell is those units plus about a day of the hauler's own, over the region's units sold per day.
- **Demand is read from the destination _region's_ daily history, the ladders from the hub _station's_ orders.** ESI has no per-station history. The row detail says so. An item counts as "rarely sells" when it traded on fewer than 8 of the last 30 days, or averaged under 2 orders per trading day (one trader, not a market).
- **The default filters are Sells within 14 days, Margin over 3%, Steady demand only.** Filtered rows are counted in the footer, never dropped silently. The thresholds are placeholders in `HAULING_THRESHOLDS` (`src/engine/market/haulingMarket.ts`), not tuned figures.
- **A margin above 100% is flagged "Check history", not hidden.** Public data cannot tell a real gap from a price held up by one seller, so the row stays and says so.
- **A scan is bounded to one market category and two cheap passes before any order book.** Fuzzwork aggregates at both hubs, then region history, then order books for the best 40 only, so a scan stays inside the ESI request budget (ADR 0003). Ships are left out of the category list because their hauled (packaged) volume is not in the catalogue and a wrong volume would mislead the number a Trip Plan rests on.
- **Margin is after market fees only.** Buying is an instant purchase from sell orders, so only the sale pays broker fee and sales tax. Trip cost, a Red Frog comparison and route risk are deferred, not rejected.
- **Cargo Space and budget are optional and device-local.** The page works with neither: quantities stop at a week of sales and at what is profitable to buy. Choosing a ship (its base hold), a saved Fitting (the exact hold) or a typed m³ adds the hold meter and lets the plan stop at what fits. An empty budget means no limit. Detecting the currently flown ship is deferred: it needs a scope the app does not request.
- **The Trip Plan is a suggestion the user edits.** Every row starts selected at a suggested quantity; unticking or typing a quantity re-sizes the rest through the same `planTrip`. The plan names which limit applied to each quantity (sales, supply, space, budget, or "you set this").
- **The page carries no permission prompt of its own.** Missing standings already raise the shell's banner; a second copy inside the panel read as a third place asking to log in. The fees line names the levels used instead.
