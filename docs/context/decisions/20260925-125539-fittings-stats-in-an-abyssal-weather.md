# Scope decisions — Fittings: stats in an Abyssal weather

_Recorded 2026-09-25._

- **The stats can be worked out inside an Abyssal weather** — Dark, Electrical, Exotic, Firestorm or Gamma at strength 1–3 — picked beside the stats ("Abyssal weather: Normal space" by default). The engine applies the weather's own beacon (its type id, projected as the pocket does), so every stat moves as it would in the pocket: each weather's resist penalty against one damage type grows with the strength (30 / 50 / 70%; Dark's is to turret optimal and falloff instead), beside a fixed bonus (Dark +50% velocity, Electrical halves capacitor recharge time, Exotic +50% scan resolution, Firestorm +50% armor HP, Gamma +50% shield HP). Pinned by a real-engine test: each weather moves its bonus and its damage type (or turret range) and nothing else, and the strengths differ. The picker says what the chosen weather does, and the stats heading names it.
- **One pick for the session, everywhere the numbers are.** The editor's stats, its Variations, the applied-DPS overlay, Fitting Compare and the logged-out share view all use it, so no two numbers on screen are in different weathers. It is not saved and not put in a Share Link: it is a question asked of a fit, not part of it, and a reload is back in normal space.
- **Not built: EVE Workbench's "maximum suggested Abyssal difficulty".** It is EVE Workbench's own scoring with no published formula; inventing thresholds would put a verdict on screen that nothing stands behind. With the weather pick, the numbers a pilot would judge that by — EHP, resists, cap, DPS in the pocket — are all there.
