# Scope decisions — Fittings page: Start screen, new from a hull, three-column editor with a slide-out browser

_Recorded 2026-09-24._

- **With nothing open, the Fittings page is a Start screen, and a Fitting can start from a bare hull.** "New fitting from a hull" (search, or browse the Ships market groups by class) is the lead path; Import (EFT, links, EVE XML), My Fittings and In-game Fittings sit beside it. A phone shows them one tab at a time. Previously a Fitting had to be Loaded before anything could be edited. The picker offers no race filter and no "I can fly" filter: the market tree groups advanced hulls by role, not race, and a per-hull skill check across every hull is out of proportion for a picker.
- **Once a Fitting is open, everything that opens a different one moves behind a Fittings menu.** The Load card and the two fitting lists no longer stack above the editor; the menu reopens the same Start content in a dialog. The header is one row, as in mockup A: the Fitting's identity (the hull beneath the name unless it is just the hull's name, and whether it is saved), what its numbers assume (implants, missing skills — a popover, so the row never grows), then the Fittings menu, Compare, Export and Save — a split button, "Save" or "Update", whose caret holds Save to EVE. Ring | List is not in the header: it is a row of the app's ordinary tabs over the column it switches. There is no separate strip of headline numbers: each stats section's row carries its own.
- **Three columns, as in the game's own window: module browser | Ring or List | stats.** The browser is the Add panel, docked on the left when the screen is wide enough for all three (100rem): Modules, Charges and Drones tabs; a banner naming the chosen slot; and a market-group tree cut down to what fits the open ship — every fittable item is checked against the hull once, in the background — and the chosen slot, with single-child groups merged. The Charges tab loads a charge into every fitted module of a type. The stats column is one panel of collapsible rows, each carrying its headline number, with Defense as a resist table (a row per layer, a column per damage type); what #1545, #1546 and #1548 put there is all kept. (A two-column focus layout with the browser only on demand was mocked up and dropped for this.)
- **Too narrow for three columns, the browser becomes a slide-out from the left; below desktop, the mobile layout.** The slide-out is non-modal so the page stays live: another slot click retargets it, and an item drags out of it onto the Ring (`20260924-205720`). It pushes the page aside instead of covering it, so no slot sits under it — it cannot get out of the way mid-drag instead, because moving or hiding a drag's source element cancels the drag. Below desktop width: Ring / List / Stats as one row of tabs — the Ring | List switch is not a separate control there — and Add as a sheet.
- **Below desktop the header is two short lines.** The Fitting's identity, Save and one ⋮ menu (the Fittings menu's items, then Export's) come first. Under them sit the implants chip and the missing-skills chip. The implants choice is a single chip reading "Implants: My clone" or "Implants: Fitting's", at every width. It opens the choice and the set editor, so its label never wraps away from its value.
- **Drones show only on a hull that takes them.** A hull with neither a drone bay nor drone bandwidth (a Corax) has no Drones tab, List section, bandwidth bar, Ring gauge or stats section. Drones already in the Fitting (from a pasted fit) keep those showing, so they can be removed.
- **The drone bay caps drones.** Adding a drone, or raising a drone count in the List, stops at what the bay holds beside the other drones there. Drones in space count against the bay, because they launched from it. A drone that won't fit is greyed out in the browser. The List shows the bay's m³ used out of total.
- **The browser shows only modules the ship can use.** On top of the hull's rules, it hides a module that needs more CPU, powergrid or calibration than the bare hull has, with the pilot's skills applied. Such a module could never go online, even with nothing else fitted. A module that only overflows what's left of the current fit still shows: the resource bar flashes when it's added. Other resources, such as the drone bay or charge capacity, don't hide anything. The variations list still shows every sibling and marks the ones that don't fit, because it is for comparing.
- **In the List, a module's or drone's name opens its info, the Market's item detail.** Variations get their own button on the card.
- **The Ring has the drones too.** On desktop and tablet, a Drones panel sits under the Ring. It shows the bandwidth and bay bars and, for each drone type, how many are launched (these count toward DPS) and how many are in the bay. On a phone, a Drones button among the rack buttons opens the same controls as a sheet. Both show only on hulls that take drones.
- **Tapping a module on the Ring opens that module.** The dialog is titled with its name and leads with its state and ammo. Variations fold under "Swap for a variation", with their count shown.
