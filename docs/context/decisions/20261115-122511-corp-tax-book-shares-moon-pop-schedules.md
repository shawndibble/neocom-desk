# Scope decisions — Corp Tax Book shares moon pop schedules

_Recorded 2026-11-15 · grilling session with the owner; no issue yet._

- **Pop Sharing is a Corp Tax Book feature and needs Automatic sync.** The book already holds the Extractions server-side, its moon owners' included, and refreshes them every 6 hours. Rules out a separate record published from an officer's device, which updates only when an officer visits. If sync stops, published Shared Pops keep showing with their data age: a started Extraction's times do not change.
- **Off by default. Book editors set it; read-only book officers see it.** Each change is kept in the book's history with who and when. Settings live in a new Sharing tab on the Moons page: the Sharing default on top, every moon the book handles below, each opening its overrides.
- **Settings: audiences (multi-select), Pop Time, ore make-up shown, start-link rate, notes.** Each is overridden per moon on its own (Moon override); the rest follow the Sharing default.
- **Corp and Alliance are always the book's own corporation and alliance, never a moon owner's.** Players keep moon-holding corporations unconnected to the accounting corporation to avoid war declarations, so the moon owner's corporation is not a natural audience. A moon owner's own pilots see a pop only through those audiences or a Community.
- **Membership is checked live through public ESI.** A pilot sees a Shared Pop when any of their Characters is currently in an audience; a pilot who leaves the corporation or alliance loses it. No public link: logged-out viewers see nothing.
- **A Community share holds only while an officer of the book's corporation is a Member there.** An officer is a Member Character in the book's corporation that used the book with an editing role in the last 30 days, with no failed role check since (officer records last an hour, so they cannot be the test). Only the book's officers count, never a moon owner's. With none, the Community stays in the settings marked "No officer in this Community" and shares nothing until one is a Member again. A Community admin can block a sharing corporation from their Community.
- **Every moon the book handles may be shared, a moon owner's included, unless that moon owner opts out.** One switch in the moon owner's moon settings, on by default, all of their moons. The opt-out is final: their pops stop at once; the book's settings for those moons are kept, inert, and come back if the owner opts in again. A share icon (tooltip: the audiences) and an opted-out icon mark moons on the Extractions tab, the Overview moons panel, the Moon extractions widget, the Moon owners tab and the Sharing tab; an unshared moon has none.
- **Pop Time is Ready or Auto-fracture, one per moon, never both.** Corporations that let the chunk break on its own want the time mining can start, not the time it could be fired. A chunk fractured early moves to its actual time once synced.
- **The Calendar gains a "Shared moon pop" Clock Kind** with its own colour and icon, and a filter by sharing corporation. When the pilot can read the same moon from ESI, the corp `moonChunk` row wins and the Shared Pop is hidden. A Shared Pop shows from the Extraction's start until it is Expired, so a late fleet still sees the field.
- **What a Shared Pop shows:** the refinery name (it carries the system, and is where pilots warp), the moon name as a second line, Pop Time and countdown, ore make-up when shared (never ISK), notes, and a Survey start link filled with the refinery, the book's corporation as payee and the rate. The link is built when shown, so a settings change reaches every link.
- **A new Notification Event, Shared Moon Pop, as a Scheduled Push.** Fires once at the Pop Time, or a per-Character lead time before it (default: at the Pop Time). On by default; Settings can mute one sharing corporation. Title "Moon pop · [Refinery]", body "[Pop Time] at [time] · taxed by [corporation] · [rate]%"; opening it opens that Shared Pop on the Calendar.
- **Open, decided at build time:** how the server stores Shared Pops for non-officers to read (an ADR if it is a new Firestore read model).
