# Scope decisions — Hauling trip rail, fill from other categories, item detail in the rail (issue #3878)

_Recorded 2026-10-15 · issue #3878._

Narrows "The category filter is respected" in
`20261004-101406-cargo-space-counts-specialised-holds-whose-contents-fit.md`:
the scan stays one category, but a **Trip Plan** may now also hold lines the
user added from other categories.

- **The trip lives in a rail beside the table.** On a wide panel a sticky rail holds the **Trip Plan**'s totals (Cargo Space, cargo value, free m³, profit, spend, profit per jump, the limit that stopped it) and Copy multibuy, so they never scroll away. Where the panel is too narrow for the rail (by its own width, ADR 0017), a sticky bottom bar carries free m³, profit and Copy multibuy, and the rest opens as a sheet.
- **Fill the free space from other categories, on demand.** _(One scan, other categories only and skipped for Everything: superseded by `20261015-224700`, which fills in batches from every category until the hold is full.)_ When the plan leaves room, the rail offers to find items from the other **Hauling Opportunities** categories on the same lane that fit it. The scan runs only when the user asks (a button), never on its own: it costs the same ESI budget as a category scan (`20260926-161750`, ADR 0003). It scans only type ids outside the current category, so its order-book slots go to the items it can offer. It is offered only with a Cargo Space set and both ends a fixed Trade Hub: it is skipped when the category is already "Everything", and with **Any hub** at either end, where it would scan every hub's books instead of the one lane. It uses the user's days, margin and demand filters.
- **The current plan is pinned while the fill is sized.** Each line the plan already ships keeps its quantity; the found items share only the space and ISK left. What a fill line shows (m³, spend, profit) is what adding it would load.
- **Added items join the Trip Plan.** Ticked fill items join the table and the plan like any scanned row, so Copy multibuy and CSV include them. They reset when the route, category or mode changes, like the user's other edits, and on the page's reload: the reload re-prices the lane, and the fill was priced from the old books. The Overview's Hauling widget still reads the main scan only (`20261011-153657`).
- **Profit per jump uses the user's route preference.** Jumps between the two Trade Hubs come from the app's route engine and the user's route preference, labelled with it. With **Any hub** at either end each row has its own lane, so no single figure is shown.
- **Clicking a row selects it; its detail shows in the rail.** The inline expand is gone. A selected row shows the selected treatment and a second click clears it (DESIGN.md §6c). On a phone the detail is a full-height sheet. The detail leads with what a hauler checks: the most to pay, the destination's sell orders with the user's own listing among them, and Days to Sell; the price derivation and fee math fold away behind "Show the math".
- **The row ⋮ and the row's right-click menu are removed.** Every item action they held (Add to Quickbar, price alert, Set stock target…, Show info, Open in EVE, Add to Compare, View in Market, Copy name, Build Plan, and PI plan where the item has one) moves to the item detail's own `ItemMoreActions` ⋮ in its header; Show info, the destination's price history and Route Safety are also plain links in the detail.
