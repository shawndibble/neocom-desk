# Scope decisions — PI Plan tab becomes a goal planner measured against a baseline

_Recorded 2026-10-04._

- **The Plan tab is replaced by a Goal Plan over the pilot's own colonies.** The old tab costed one product, planet-agnostically, against a sourcing floor and a layout. It answered "what does a P4 cost to make" but not what a PI pilot opens the page to ask: "what should my planets make", "how do I make X", "given my planets and these goals, what's the plan". Four mockups and a veteran-player review settled on one merged tool; this is milestone 1, the planner (question C, and B for a single goal). A chain-browser tier map (B) and an opportunity ranking (A) follow on the same model. Retired with the old tab: the tax-sensitivity grid, the CSV export and the sourcing-floor control — a hub setting (buy or make-everything) replaces the floor.
- **Every ISK figure is a Lift over the Baseline, never a gross margin.** The reviewer's top finding across every mockup was the same error: extracted P0 priced at zero, so any chain looked like +4M/day when the same planets selling P1 already earned most of that. The Baseline is each colony selling the best P1 it can make alone, at its own customs rate, priced at the hub bid after sales tax. A plan identical to the Baseline has Lift zero by construction.
- **The Goal Plan may re-plan extraction, as an explicit change list.** ADR 0012 keeps the Advisor from re-planning extraction, because a recommendation must not rest on a second recommendation the pilot has not taken. The Goal Plan is the opt-in what-if that does exactly that, so it is presented as changes against today's pins ("re-target Uttindar V from Reactive Gas to Base Metals") and a re-targeted extraction rate is always badged as an estimate. The Advisor is unchanged.
- **A colony may extract one or two P0 types.** One P0 per planet was the second-biggest modelling error the review found: it turns ordinary two-ECU colonies into false shortfalls. A colony runs up to two ECUs, on one P0 or two, fitted together against its CPU/Powergrid; a second ECU on the same P0 is credited at `DEFAULT_EXTRA_EXTRACTOR_YIELD_FACTOR`.
- **Extraction rates come from the pilot's own programs, then an editable fallback.** A colony's measured program for that P0 (re-projected at the restart cadence) first, else the mean of the pilot's measured programs, else a typed fallback. Richness is scanner-only, so the last two are labelled estimates.
- **The solver is greedy and says so.** Scarcest P0 first, factory host chosen as the eligible colony whose extraction is least needed. Not optimal; the shortfalls and the change list are what make it checkable.
- **Out of scope for milestone 1:** the tier map, the opportunity ranking, stock on hand, alt characters' colonies (each character's pool is planned separately later), and an SVG flow diagram (a tiered table stands in).
