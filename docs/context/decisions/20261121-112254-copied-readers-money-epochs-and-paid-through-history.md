# Scope decisions — Copied readers, money epochs and paid-through history

_Recorded 2026-11-21._

- **Rows and payments keep a copy of their readers (`uids`), kept current by a durable queue.** The rules cannot follow a Character to its grouping, so each `observed` row and payment holds its readers. Any act that changes who reads a Miner adds the affected Characters (their rows) and Miners (their payments) to `corps/{id}/readersQueue/pending`, in the act's own transaction. These acts are a grouping, a regroup, an unlink, a guest's confirm or decline, a declined link, a promoted claim and a rekey. The act does not rewrite rows or payments itself: one Character can hold more of them than a transaction writes. A drain does the rewriting (`corpTaxBookReaders.ts`). It runs right after the act, after every sync and nightly, until the queue is empty. It works 400 records a page, re-reads the groupings for each page, and guards each write with the record's update time. Each record it changes gets a fresh `updatedAt`. It clears an entry only once that entry is done and nobody marked it again. This rules out refusing a regroup for its payment count, and rows left stale when the one best-effort pass failed.
- **A readers epoch tells a device to start over.** `corps/{id}.readersEpoch` (a number, absent = 0) moves whenever a drain takes a reader off a record, and whenever the backfill gives records readers. A device whose delta read last ran under another epoch reads its rows and payments whole again. Otherwise it would keep records it may no longer read, or miss ones it now may.
- **The backfill repairs, not only fills.** It rewrites any row or payment whose `uids` differ from what the groupings say now, stamps `updatedAt` on each, and moves the readers epoch.
- **Each Miner's money has an epoch, and a tab's figures must name the one they were worked out under.** `meta/paid.bookEpoch` moves with every edit that may reach every Miner. `meta/paid.minerEpochs.<minerId>` moves with every edit of that Miner's money or checkpoint (whatever its date) and every paid-through release, in the edit's own transaction. A Miner's epoch is the two added (`moneyEpochOf`). A checkpoint (`recordCheckpoints`) or a paid-through day (`recordAccounts`' `settledThrough`) that names another epoch is skipped as `stale` and recorded again on the next load: a tab that read the money before an edit never records figures the edit changed. An older tab that sends no epoch is checked as before. A stored checkpoint keeps the epoch it stands for.
- **`recordAccounts` audits what it stored:** the accounts it wrote, the paid-through days it advanced, and the Miners it refused as stale. A day forgiven by a recorded figure is therefore always in the audit log. Write-offs stay editor-level and audited (accepted within the editor trust model).
