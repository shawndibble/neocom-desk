# Scope decisions — Price fallback when Fuzzwork is down

_Recorded 2026-10-21. Amends ADR 0002. Hub order-book step and R2 cap added 2026-11-13 (#6138)._

- **Fuzzwork stays the primary price source; three sources sit behind it.** A lookup that throws, or whose whole batch comes back with no price on any type, falls through to (1) ESI region orders folded to the station, then (2) the hub's order books in R2, then (3) the daily open prices in R2. One seam: `fetchAggregatesWithFallback` in `src/market/priceFallback.ts`, called from `prices.ts`. `fuzzwork.ts` is unchanged.
- **ESI step: exact and live, but small.** One `GET /markets/{region}/orders?type_id=` per type, at most 50 types and 4 at a time. Orders are filtered to the station (`location_id`), so the result matches Fuzzwork's station aggregate. All or nothing: a failed or truncated book abandons the step, because a partial book quotes a wrong best price. A non-hub station resolves to its region through the SDE snapshots; a player structure or an unreadable snapshot skips the step. Region scope (`getRegionSellPrices`) uses the same step unfiltered.
- **Order-book step: one read per hub, any batch size, flagged stale.** `hauling-books/<stationId>.json` (the 30-minute EVE Ref job that feeds the hauling scan, `loadHaulingBooks`) holds every type's best 30 levels a side at each Trade Hub station, so one cached read prices a whole batch: the best level is the price, the kept levels' units are the volume (a floor). Books older than 3 hours are not used. Added because the per-type R2 step made one read per type (~1,850 reads, ~16 s for a hauling scan with Fuzzwork down). Hub stations only, never region scope.
- **R2 step: yesterday's close, hub stations only, at most 200 types, flagged stale.** Used when the books are missing or have no price for the batch. The last day of each type's open-price series (`open-prices/shards/<typeId % 4096>.json`, then the legacy `open-prices/<typeId>.json`); no volumes (0). One read per type, 10 at a time, so a batch over 200 types skips it: with the books also down, such a batch reads unreachable and the page shows its "prices unavailable" state. Never used for region scope (it holds the hub station's price, not the region's lowest). A type is `stale`, not cached in memory or Dexie, so the next ask retries Fuzzwork.
- **`PricesWithStatus.stale`.** `unreachable` now means no source answered. The Appraisal shows one amber line (`market.appraisal.pricesFromSnapshot`) when its prices are stale (from either R2 step). Other pages do not label it yet; they show the figure as before.
- **Callers that compare with a live order refuse stale prices.** Price alerts, the market-orders poll and the undercut/competition read pass `allowStale: false`; a stale type reads as unreachable there, so yesterday's close cannot fire a false alert or undercut.
- **A close older than 3 days is not offered.** If the daily job stopped, the "yesterday" label would lie, so the R2 step answers nothing and the lookup reads unreachable.
- **ESI-sourced prices are live, so they are cached on the normal 15-minute TTL**, which means they can outlast Fuzzwork's recovery by up to that long. Only stale (R2: order books or yesterday's close) prices skip the cache.
- **Outage is remembered for 60 s.** After Fuzzwork throws, lookups go straight to the fallback so each ask does not wait out the 15 s timeout.
- **Order counts have an ESI step only** (`fetchOrderCountsWithFallback`): R2 holds no counts.
- **Not changed:** the nightly SDE refresh still reads Fuzzwork's CSV dump; a failure there leaves the baked data in place and shows nothing to users. Janice, EVE Tycoon and Adam4EVE stay rejected (ADR 0002: no CORS or a key in the bundle).
