# Scope decisions — Pilot profile and Local list state a Threat verdict

_Recorded 2026-10-08._

- **A pilot's profile and Local list row now carry a Threat verdict: a colour-coded badge beside the name.** The question the user brings to a pilot is "how dangerous could they be?", and ten numbers do not answer it at a glance. This reverses "Numbers, never a verdict: no pilot is called hostile or safe" (`20260912-172628`), which is why the copy guard in `PilotLookupPanel.test.tsx` no longer forbids the word "threat".
- **The levels are Dangerous (red), Active (amber), Low threat (neutral) and Inactive (dim).** There is no green level and no word "safe": the verdict reads kills only, so it can say a pilot looks threatening and never that one is harmless. The copy guard still forbids "safe", "hostile" and "avoid", apart from the help text's "never means safe".
- **The rule is `threatVerdict` (`src/engine/pilotList/threatVerdict.ts`).** Only kills in the last 90 days count. None is Inactive; 3 or more is Active; 10 or more together with zKillboard's all-time danger ratio of 50 or more is Dangerous; otherwise Low threat. A missing danger ratio can never reach Dangerous. Losses are not part of it: only a pilot's newest 25 kills and losses are fetched, so there is no recent-losses window to read.
- **The Local list looks up stats only for pilots who could be Dangerous.** The danger ratio only matters at 10 or more kills in 90 days, so `needsDangerRatio` gates a `fetchPilotStats` call per row. Every other row's verdict is already final from the kill history the list loads anyway, so most pilots cost no extra request. This narrows "Those two (danger/gang ratios) stay in the pilot's modal" (`20261008-143948`): the gang ratio still stays there, and the danger ratio is read for the badge only.
- **The meters keep zKillboard's own Snuggly to Dangerous scale** (`20261002-145207`) and their colours by side (`20261002-163430`). The verdict does not replace them; it sits above them. The ISK efficiency tile is dropped from a pilot's figures: the danger ratio already is kills against losses, and efficiency repeated it in ISK.
