# Scope decisions — One card per corporation, bare marks are not money, archived Payees

_Recorded 2026-11-22._

- **One card per corporation.** A personal Payee identified by its entity id (kind corporation, or none recorded), or with no entity id at all and named exactly as the corporation (EVE names are unique; case-insensitive), as the corporation of a Corp Tax Book the pilot is in has no account that counts: no card, no credit, no Settle up, never offered a payment to link. The book's card and ledger are the only figures. The Payee's old history stays viewable, read-only and with no running balance, from Manage Payees ("Tracked by {corp}'s Corp Tax Book now", "Old history"): the lightest way, since it needs no section inside the book's ledger. Rules out folding the old rows into the book's ledger.
- **A bare "Mark paid" is the pilot's word, not money.** A legacy link with no payment behind it (`mark:<assignmentId>`) settles only its own day, after every real payment has paid oldest first, for no more than it said and than is left of that day. It never pays another day and never makes credit. Read at build time, so existing data corrects itself with no migration. It shows in the ledger as "Marked paid" with what it settled, possibly nothing. It stays a link: once the real payment is linked it simply settles 0.
- **Archive a Payee.** `archivedAt` on the Payee record (synced). A Payee reads archived only while nothing is owed and none of its billed Assignments was written (`updatedAt`) after `archivedAt` (by when it was entered, never the day it was mined, so a backfilled or same-day entry covered by credit shows it too): derived, never written, so it shows itself again when a Default System bills it, with no race between devices. An archived Payee is never offered a payment to link, in the suggestions or the manual pick: the pilot hid it on purpose, and Unarchive is in Manage Payees. Archived: no card, credit counts nowhere, out of pickers and suggestions; its history and ledger stay, under "Archived" in Manage Payees, with Unarchive.
