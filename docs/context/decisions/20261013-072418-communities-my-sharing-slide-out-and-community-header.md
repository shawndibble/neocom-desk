# Scope decisions — Communities: My sharing slide-out and Community header card

_Recorded 2026-10-13. Supersedes the Everyone | Mine `SegmentedControl` and remembered-pick bullets of `20261012-224358-communities-page-leads-with-shared-content.md`; its other bullets stand._

The owner found the open Community too boxy: a titled panel held the tabs, an Everyone | Mine switch, a sentence-long BPC Sourcing link and a second row of filter chips before any data. Three mockups were compared; the owner picked "Identity card" (concept B).

- **The open Community is a header card, not a titled panel.** Initials tile, name, the Admin badge for its admin, up to five Member portraits (the uid carries the character id, so `CharacterAvatar` works) and the first three names with "and N more", and the admin's Manage menu in the card's top-right corner. The tabs sit on the page under it; each tab's content is a titleless `Panel`.
- **The Member count is a round badge on the Members tab** (`Tabs`' new `count`, a §6c cue). The card does not repeat it.
- **Everyone | Mine is gone. Each content tab shows the Community's content under a sharing line**: one sentence on what this Character shares there and a My sharing button that opens that tab's share controls (the old Mine view, unchanged) in a `SlideOver`. The line reads only what is already on hand, so it costs no read: the blueprint choice and the Fittings share-all switch on this device, and this Character's own rows in the Fittings and Skill plans lists the tab already loaded. Under share-all for blueprints it says "all your blueprints" with no number, since counting would need the owned blueprints from ESI.
- **The slide-out is non-modal and follows the tab.** Switching between content tabs while it is open shows that tab's controls; switching to Members closes it. Closing it remounts the tab's list and rereads the line, since sharing or stopping changed what the list shows. A Modal opened inside it (Add, a confirm) now takes its own Escape alone: `SlideOver` ignores an Escape from inside an open `<dialog>`.
- **The Everyone / Mine pick is no longer remembered.** `communityContentScope` left the "Reset saved view preferences" list; a stored row is harmless and ignored. An old `?view=mine` link opens My sharing on the tab it names.
- **The Community Library's four filter chips became one Show select** (Radix `Select`, each option with its count), beside the search. BPC Sourcing is a button at the row's end with an `InfoTooltip` (the "i" glyph) holding the sentence that used to sit on the page.
- **Create says "Create"** (its accessible name stays "Create a Community") and is always `size="md"`, the header's height, level with the alerts bell. The page intro shows only on the list, not inside a Community, whose card already says what it is. The switcher is `sm:w-72` instead of full width.
