# Scope decisions — Items board flags overpriced single-type contracts, silent on bundles and unknowns (issue #4773)

_Recorded 2026-10-23 · issue #4773._

- **The Items board flags a contract that asks far above the hub, and says nothing when it cannot tell.** A marker before the price states the condition and its multiple, never a verdict, as the courier risk flags do (`20260912-172628-courier-risk-flags-state-a-condition-never-a.md`). Silent: no hub price yet, a barter or unpriced PLEX ask, an auction with no buyout, a contract that also asks for items, or a contract with other lines.
- **A bundle is never flagged on its row.** A Multi-Type Contract's price is never attributed to one line (`20260915-093419-multi-type-contract-price-treated-as-unknowable-never.md`). The board reads only the rows of the types searched, so it cannot see a bundle from its rows. The sync now stamps every offer row with `lineCount`, the for-sale lines in its contract (one per stack, so two stacks of one type are two lines: the one price pays for both), and a row without it (a publish from before the field) is unknown, never single-line. The detail slide-over, which holds every line, words a minority share (under half the value at the hub) as a sentence instead.
- **Rows are held against two prices.** A plain item against the Trade Hub's best sell for every unit it carries. A blueprint copy against the original's lowest sell in the hub's region, since an NPC-seeded original is rarely sold at the hub station itself. Both come through `market/prices` on its existing cache: no new endpoint, no new ESI request.
- **One setting, presets only.** `Flag item contracts at`: 5, 10, 20 or 50 times the hub sell, default 10 (Market Browser's bait factor), synced like the courier collateral multiple. 3× stays out: contract prices carry a convenience premium and it would flag honest listings. A copy is flagged against its original whatever it says.
- **Hide overpriced hides what the marker marks, and says so.** An opt-in chip in the filter funnel (Market Browser's Hide bait sells, `20261010-172632-market-browser-hide-bait-sells-toggle.md`), off by default, URL-backed. It drops only flagged rows, so a bundle or an unknown is never hidden, and prints "N overpriced contracts hidden". The board prices at most 100 distinct types at once; a wider browse stays unflagged rather than lag.
- **The line count needs a Cloud Functions deploy.** Functions are not auto-deployed, so row flags stay silent until the next publish after deploy. The modal sentences work at once.
