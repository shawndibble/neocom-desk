# Scope decisions — Cargo Space counts specialised holds whose contents fit (issue #2450)

_Recorded 2026-10-04 · issue #2450._

Supersedes the "not specialised holds" half of
`20261003-095644-cargo-space-counts-the-cargo-hold-and-fleet.md`; the cargo
hold and fleet hangar still take any item and together make the general hold.

- **Cargo Space is a set of holds, not one number.** A ship's (base hull or saved **Fitting**) Cargo Space is its general hold (cargo hold plus fleet hangar, which take anything) and every **Specialised Hold** it has from the list below. Each hold keeps its own capacity, so `planTrip` can tell which one is full and which still has room for a certain kind of item.
- **A Trip Plan fills Specialised Holds first.** An item goes into a Specialised Hold that accepts it before it uses the general hold, and spills into the general hold once that Specialised Hold is full. An item no Specialised Hold accepts only ever uses the general hold. A Trip Plan is still one plan for one trip; it just sizes each item against the space it can actually use rather than one total.
- **Hauling Opportunities shows each hold separately**, one fill bar per hold, so a hauler sees which hold is full and which has room.
- **A typed Cargo Space is one hold of a chosen kind.** The manual Cargo Space popup gains a select beside the m³: "Any item" (the default, and what every existing typed value means) or one of the Specialised Hold kinds below, in which case only items that hold accepts can go in it.
- **The category filter is respected.** When the scan is filtered to a category, the plan only considers candidates from that category, and only the holds that accept them can take them. A hold no candidate fits shows as empty, not as spare space.
- **What a hold accepts is a hand-maintained table, not game data.** Hold _capacities_ are dogma attributes in the SDE. What each hold _accepts_ is not in the SDE, ESI or Hoboleaks; it lives in client code (`inventoryFlagData` / `ShouldAllowAdd` in `inventoryFlagsCommon.py`, public only as a 2016 decompile, <https://github.com/connoryang/dec-eve-serenity>). So the app keeps its own table of category/group IDs per hold, with the sources below, and updates it from patch notes.
- **When unsure, leave it out.** Every rule below has a named source; the Confidence column says how direct it is. High means CCP code, patch notes or a player test of that item. Medium means the source names the kind of item (the Infrastructure tooltip's "Upwell Structures", players reporting what the Kryos ice hold refuses) and the ID is the SDE group or category that kind maps to. Anything with no source at all is left out of the Specialised Hold and counts only toward the general hold. The plan may understate a load but never plans one that doesn't fit.

The Infrastructure hold explicitly rejects cap boosters, ordinary missiles,

P0 and regular fighters.

## Left out, and why

- **Compressed gas (group 4168, category 2 Celestial, so the Mining hold's category 25 rule doesn't cover it)** in the Gas or Mining hold, and **Fuel Blocks (group 1136)** in a ship's Fuel bay: no source confirms either. They count toward the general hold only until someone confirms them.
- **Corpse, Booster, Subsystem, Mobile Depot (the ship hold, not the item), Expedition, Quafe, Salvage and Material holds**: too small, on no hauler, or with unknown rules.
- **Ship holds** (ship maintenance bay, ship hangars, frigate escape bay): take ships only.
- **Asteroid hold** (3227): no published ship has one.
