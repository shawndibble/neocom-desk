# Scope decisions — Moon owner refineries: archive hides, offered by rule R3, mining restores (issue #6298)

_Recorded 2026-11-17 · issue #6298._

- **Archive hides; it never deletes.** An archived refinery leaves the moon owner page's Refineries list and shows under "Archived (N)" with when, who, why and Restore. Its Extractions, mining rows, label and Late notices stay, and every figure still counts its mining. Stored additively on the moon owner's record as `refineryArchive.{structureId}` (`{ at, by, reason }`); a record without it has nothing archived. A restore deletes the entry.
- **The page offers Archive by rule R3.** It offers (amber pill on the row, accent button and a hint on the refinery page) when ESI shows the structure gone, or when there has been no Extraction and no mining for 60 days (`REFINERY_IDLE_DAYS`, measured from the later of the last Extraction start or chunk arrival and the last mined day). An officer may always archive by hand from the refinery page; the rule only decides the offer. Rejected: R1 alone (never fires when the grant lacks the structure scope) and R2 alone (a paused refinery looks gone).
- **"ESI shows it gone" is the structure lookup being refused.** The sync's `/universe/structures` read stores `denied` for a 403 or a 404 and cannot tell no-docking-access from gone, so a refused lookup is the signal. A wrong offer costs one reversible click.
- **Mining after the archive day restores it by itself.** Derived on read, nothing written: an archive stops counting once a covered mining row at the refinery has a UTC day after the archive's day. Mining on or before that day, or a new Extraction alone, does not.
- **One callable, a router action.** `setCorpTaxBookRefineryArchiveCallable` (accounting officers, live role check, `edit`) archives or restores; it refuses a refinery with no observed row or Extraction from that moon owner (`unknown-refinery`) and a moon owner that left or was removed. Audited as `refineryArchived` / `refineryRestored`.
- **The refinery page lives at `/corp/moons/moon-owners/:owner/refineries/:structureId`.** Below its moon owner, because a refinery is the moon owner's; the moon link on it goes to the moon's page (#6299). No ⋮ anywhere: Rename (the pencil) and Archive are not two real actions for a menu.
