# Scope decisions — A corporation's own Payee is assignable again; Settling waits 20 minutes

_Recorded 2026-11-25._

- **A Payee a Corp Tax Book is the authority for is offered in the picker and suggestions again.** This supersedes 20261122-030058's "out of pickers". An open day is owed at an estimate only for ore the member gives the corporation's own Payee (20261125-171154). With that Payee hidden, a member couldn't say "today's mining is Bureau's". Giving it ore creates no account of its own, so the one-card rule still holds: the book bills the day, and its card and ledger are the only figures. Archived Payees stay out. Default Systems still never assign it on their own (`applyDefaultSystems`). A suggestion only pre-fills a dialog the member confirms, but an automatic assignment could silently give the book another owner's moon in the same system. Ore the member leaves unassigned is still read as Pending corp record and taken by the book when the day closes.
- **Settling ends 20 minutes after the day closes (end of the UTC day plus the book's grace), not 24 hours after it ended.** It still ends sooner if the book records the miner's ore at that refinery on a later day. Per the user, who mines with these logs daily, the refinery log runs a few minutes behind, ten at most. Ore still missing after that is a real difference the member needs to see as a Conflict. A late arrival still folds into the billed row whenever it comes. This supersedes the 24-hour cap in 20261124-211423, and the six-hour and two-hour caps that briefly stood here.
- **An open day's estimate offers Edit and Split on the member's own entry it stands in for.** These appear in its detail (the Edit split button any row has) and in its ⋮ menu, until the book bills the day. They let the member split off ore that was another Payee's, such as a non-book moon in the same system, before the day closes. Once billed, the book's rows are the book's.
- **A day that's over, and that the book's sync since midnight holds in full, is final before the book bills it.** On the member's side only, once the UTC day has ended and the book's latest sync holds all the ore the member gave the corporation's Payee, the estimate counts as final. The refinery log never records more than the member's own log, so nothing more can arrive. The banner calls the day safe to pay and drops "billed after 00:30". The book still bills it after 00:30 UTC and its next sync, because officers use that clock, and at the same figure.
