# Scope decisions — A corporation's own Payee is assignable again; Settling waits six hours

_Recorded 2026-11-26._

- **A Payee a Corp Tax Book is the authority for is offered in the picker and suggestions again.** This supersedes 20261122-030058's "out of pickers". An open day is owed at an estimate only for ore the member gives the corporation's own Payee (20261125-171154). With that Payee hidden, a member couldn't say "today's mining is Bureau's". Giving it ore creates no account of its own, so the one-card rule still holds: the book bills the day, and its card and ledger are the only figures. Archived Payees stay out.
- **Settling ends six hours after the day closes, not 24 hours after it ended.** It still ends sooner if the book records the miner's ore at that refinery on a later day. The refinery log's lag is about an hour on top of ESI's one-hour cache: 24 Nov's missing ore arrived within two hours. On 25 Nov the missing ore was still missing 20 hours later, so waiting longer only hides a real difference that the member needs to see as a Conflict. This supersedes the 24-hour cap in 20261124-211423.
