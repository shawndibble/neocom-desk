# Scope decisions — BPO cards in BPC Sourcing are two lines: NPC-seeded is a hint, ME/TE rides the system line

_Recorded 2026-10-13._

- **The Market BPO card drops its "incl. NPC-seeded" line; the caveat is an info hint beside the price.** Nothing is lost: the line was the only thing telling a player order from an NPC seed, and ESI cannot tell them apart. The card is now two lines, as tall as a Cheapest by region cell.
- **The Contract BPO card shows research as "10/20" after the system, with "ME 10 / TE 20" on hover.** An original carries its own research (ME up to 10, TE up to 20) that copies made from it inherit, so a researched original is worth far more than a blank one. A market original is always ME0/TE0, so only the contract card shows it.
