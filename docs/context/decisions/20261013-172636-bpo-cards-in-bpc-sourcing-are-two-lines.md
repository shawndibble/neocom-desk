# Scope decisions — BPO cards in BPC Sourcing are two lines: NPC-seeded is a hint, ME/TE rides the system line

_Recorded 2026-10-13._

- **The Market BPO card drops its "incl. NPC-seeded" line; the caveat is an info hint beside the price.** Nothing is lost: the line was the only thing telling a player order from an NPC seed, and ESI cannot tell them apart. The card is now two lines, as tall as a Cheapest by region cell.
- **The Contract BPO card shows research as "10/20" after the system, with "ME 10 / TE 20" on hover.** An original carries its own research (ME up to 10, TE up to 20) that copies made from it inherit, so a researched original is worth far more than a blank one. A market original is always ME0/TE0, so only the contract card shows it.
- **Cheapest by region shows the regions a pilot prices at, three at most, not every region the blueprint is listed in.** The Forge always (Jita's market is the baseline many price against), then their market hub's region (the synced Default trade hub), then the region of the trade hub fewest jumps from their Current System, then the region they are in. Each region appears once; the first three distinct win, so the current region only shows when two of the others coincide, and all coinciding leaves one card. A fixed order, not cheapest-first, so The Forge stays where the pilot looks for it; the accent marks the cheapest. A shown region with no offers says "No offers"; the strip is hidden under a Region filter. Any other region is the Region filter's job.
- **The Cheapest by region strip now always shows once a blueprint is picked (without a Region filter), even for a blueprint listed nowhere.** The Forge cell then reads "No offers" rather than the strip vanishing, because the baseline is the answer: no offers in Jita is itself the finding. The old strip appeared only for two or more listed regions.
