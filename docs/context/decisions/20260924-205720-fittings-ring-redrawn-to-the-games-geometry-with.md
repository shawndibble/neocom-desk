# Scope decisions — Fittings ring redrawn to the game's geometry, with drag-and-drop and phone rack sheets

_Recorded 2026-09-24._

- **The Ring view follows the game's fitting window (and EVE Workbench's radial), not a free layout.** One dark band with the hull's render filling the disc inside it; square slot tiles sit on the band, each rack filling clockwise from its first position — highs across the top, mids down the right, lows along the bottom, rigs upper-left. Every rack draws its full set of positions (8 high, 8 mid, 8 low, 3 rigs): the hull's own slots as tiles (dashed when free), the rest as faint outlines. CPU, powergrid, calibration and drone bandwidth are tick gauges on the outer rim, with their numbers in the corners. This replaces the zig-zag ring of `20260924-184905`, whose zig-zag existed only because the ring sat in a half-width column. Subsystems still render as a row beneath the ring.
- **On a phone the ring is an overview, and the rack buttons under it edit.** At 390px a single-track ring's tiles are about 24px — below the 44px floor — so the phone ring drops its corner readouts, its tiles only explain themselves (tap shows the tooltip), and four rack buttons (High, Mid, Low, Rigs; 44px+) open that rack's slots as a sheet of full-size rows. This supersedes `20260924-184905`'s "44px slot targets on the ring at every size"; the phone default stays List.
- **Ring tiles carry a tooltip: hover with a mouse, tap on the phone's overview ring.** Where a tap already does something (opening the module or the Add panel) the tooltip stays on touch-and-hold, as `Tooltip`'s own rule requires.
- **Modules drag straight onto the ring, as in the game.** An Add panel item dropped on a slot of its rack fits there, replacing any occupant; a fitted module dragged to another slot of its rack moves there, swapping with any occupant and keeping its state and charge. Drag is pointer-only (HTML drag and drop); clicking a slot and picking from the Add panel stays the keyboard and touch path. Every drop is one Fitting edit, so it pushes browser history like any other (`20260924-183346`).
