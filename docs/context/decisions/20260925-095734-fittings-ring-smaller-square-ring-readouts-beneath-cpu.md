# Scope decisions — Fittings ring: smaller square ring, readouts beneath, CPU and powergrid split

_Recorded 2026-09-25._

- **The Ring is a square box capped at 36rem, and its budget numbers sit in a strip beneath it rather than in its corners.** Supersedes `20260924-205720`'s "with their numbers in the corners". The corners cost the box 120 ring units of width it drew nothing in, so every tile rendered smaller than the ring's own size implied, and the Ring column needed ~41rem before the corners fitted at all — which is what kept the editor single-column below 1280px. Square and capped, the Ring view sits beside the stats from the desktop breakpoint (1024px), measured at ~29px tiles there, ~35px at 1100px, ~41px at 1280px and ~43px at the cap. The List view keeps its old xl (1280px) breakpoint: its rows need the width more than the stats do. The strip is laid out as the rim is: calibration and powergrid on the left, drone bandwidth and CPU on the right.
- **Tiles are bigger for the ring's size, and the module fills them.** 48 of 648 ring units (was 44 of 760 across), at a 12° pitch, with the icon at 88% of the tile instead of 70%. The rack order is unchanged — highs over the top, mids down the right, lows round the bottom, rigs on the left — turned slightly anticlockwise as the game window is, the four racks an even ~11° apart.
- **CPU and powergrid are separate bands, told apart by side and form, each with its numbers on hover.** CPU rises solid up the lower right, powergrid dashed up the lower left, with the bottom between them; calibration and drone bandwidth are thin dim bands above. Continuous bands replace the tick marks, which blurred together once the ring scaled down. No new colour: DESIGN.md asks a new series to try form first, and `danger` keeps meaning over budget. Hovering a band shows its used / total, share and any overage; the strip's `role="meter"` readouts stay the accessible source of the same numbers, and the gauges themselves are hidden from assistive tech. The phone overview only draws the bands — it has no strip, a band there is a sliver to tap, and its numbers are on the List bars and the Stats tab as before.
- **The Ring shows the cargo too**, as a row of icon tiles with counts beneath it, like the subsystems (the drones already have their own panel under the Ring). A Fitting's cargo is also listed and editable (quantity, remove) in the List view, which previously never showed it.
