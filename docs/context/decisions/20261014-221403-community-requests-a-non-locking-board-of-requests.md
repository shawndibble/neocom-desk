# Scope decisions — Community Requests: a non-locking board of Requests and Pledges

_Recorded 2026-10-14._

- **A Community has a Requests tab; any Member posts a Request.** A Request is one or more lines (item, quantity), an optional location (system or region) and an optional one-line terms field. It replaces the "WTB" posts Communities keep in Discord. Buying side only: the sell side is the Community Library and the "ask <name> to copy" row; a general WTS post is a later, separately named feature.
- **Lines are items, BPCs, BPOs (loans) or materials.** A BPC line may carry runs, ME and TE as the ask; a delivery with other values still counts and is flagged ("ME 8, asked 10"; superseded: ESI personal contract items carry no ME/TE, so only runs is compared, see `20261015-010948-request-delivery-marking-manual-amounts-and-write-batching.md`). Entered by pasting an Appraisal-style pile (the existing Appraisal parser) or by searching and adding items. "Request these" from an Appraisal, a Build Plan shortfall or a Fitting is a later entry point.
- **A Member Pledges a quantity of any line; nothing is locked.** Pledges are non-exclusive notes ("I can provide N of this"), not claims or reservations, so they may add up to more than the need. One editable Pledge per Member per line; a requester cannot Pledge on their own Request. A Pledge with no delivery after 14 days greys out; nothing releases automatically. The word is Pledge, not Claim, because a claim implies a hold.
- **No structured price anywhere.** A priced deal is an ordinary contract; this tool only helps Members find each other. The terms line is free text, shown in the board row. No ISK value is computed on a Request.
- **Delivery marks itself from ESI contracts, no opt-in.** A Member's app matches their own finished item-exchange contracts to the requester against their Pledges and records the delivered quantity (Request, line, quantity, contract id only; never prices or other contract contents). Both sides read the same ESI contract, so there is nothing to reconcile and no requester confirmation step. Without the contracts scope a Member types a delivered amount, marked "manual" (superseded: manual entry is always offered, and the figure shown is the smaller of the Pledged quantity and the larger of contract and manual, never the sum; see `20261015-010948-request-delivery-marking-manual-amounts-and-write-batching.md`). Marking runs when the filler's app runs.
- **Request status is derived** from Pledges and deliveries: Open, Pledged, Part delivered, Delivered. Only Closed is manual, set by the requester and reopenable. A Request expires 30 days after it is posted or last Extended (Pledges and deliveries do not push it); the requester can extend or close it. A closed Request is deleted 7 days after closing and an expired one is kept 7 days past `expiresAt`, still Expired and extendable (superseded by `20261015-005522-community-request-admin-removal-and-expiry-grace.md`).
- **Mail is first contact only.** On posting, the requester ticks recipients (pre-ticked: Members who share a matching blueprint); each gets one mail covering the whole list. Cap: 100 Request mails a day per sender, counted in mails not lines, separate from the invite cap of 20. After posting, only the in-app badge notifies. A Message button opens an in-game mail to the requester (client-side open-window call, outside the cap).
- **Visible to the whole Community**, filtered by Open, Mine, "I can fill" (a line for a blueprint I share) and "within N jumps of my pilot". The admin can remove any Request or Pledge; there is no per-Community switch.
- **Leaving deletes.** A requester leaving takes their Requests; a pledger leaving takes their Pledges (ADR 0018's rule).
- **UI is the mockup** (removed after shipping; see git history of `docs/design/mockups/community-requests/`): board, side panel (Member and requester views), compose dialog in two steps, empty state, each with a phone variant. A ticket that builds any of it implements those screens, composed from the existing `src/components/ui` primitives.

- **Known fragility, Request location (issue #3811).** A Request's location is still free text. The board parses it back by name to measure jumps: the first comma-separated token is matched as a system before the whole text is tried as a region, and a trailing "(region)" is a data format, not a label. Compose writes that format; older or hand-typed text that does not match is simply unmeasured (counted under a distance limit, never guessed). The follow-up is structured `locationSystemId` / `locationRegionId` fields on the Request, set by the picker; not built yet.
