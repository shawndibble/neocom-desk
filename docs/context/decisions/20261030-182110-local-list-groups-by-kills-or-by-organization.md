# Scope decisions — Local list groups by kills or by organization (issue #5668)

_Recorded 2026-10-30 · issue #5668. Adds a second grouping beside `20261008-143948`; it does not replace it._

- **A "Group by: Kills | Organization" switch, Kills by default.** Kills answers "can I ignore this pilot here?" (`20261008-143948`, unchanged); Organization answers "who owns this Local?". The owner chose a switch over replacing the kill groups, which would have sunk a small red corporation below a big neutral alliance. The pick is a device-local view preference (`pilotLookupLocalGrouping`), never synced, cleared by "Reset saved view preferences".
- **Organization view shape.** An alliance with two or more of its corporations here gets a heading and a sub-heading per corporation; one with a single corporation is headed by that corporation, the alliance named beside it; a corporation with no alliance heads its own pilots. A pilot with no one else from their corporation or alliance here goes under one "On their own" heading, not a heading each. A pilot whose corporation is not known yet is "Corporation not known", never "no alliance". Groups run largest first, then by name. A heading carries the EVE image-server logo, the name (Show Info), the pilot count, how many read Dangerous or Active, and your standing toward that organization. Friendly stays last and folded, as in the Kills view. On a phone each organization folds behind its heading, the largest open.
- **Every name is placed; only 40 get kills.** The paste keeps up to 2,000 names (`MAX_LOCAL_NAMES`; more are counted, "not read"). All are resolved (`/universe/ids`, batches of 500) and placed by `/characters/affiliation`; zKillboard is still asked about the first 40 only (`MAX_LIST_PILOTS`, the fan-out reason of `20261007-224716`). The rest say "Not looked up". The Kills view still lists only the first 40, as before.
- **A newest kill older than about six months (183 days) is not dated** anywhere a pilot's last kill is shown (Local list rows, the profile's "Where they kill", the Threat band's inactive line). "Last kill 390d ago" said nothing a blank does not.
- **Rules out:** a share link for a Local snapshot, a saved organization red-list (a −10 contact does it), click-to-filter by organization, raising the 40 zKillboard cap.
