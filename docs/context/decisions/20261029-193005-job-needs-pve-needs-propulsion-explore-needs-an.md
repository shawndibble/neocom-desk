# Scope decisions — Job needs: PvE needs propulsion, Explore needs an MWD, added by the Makeover as a forced first step (issue #5578)

_Recorded 2026-10-29 · issue #5578._

- **Propulsion is a job need, not a speed weight.** Slow-boating a PvE Drake is painful, but speed 188 to 444 m/s is worth only 0.25 under PvE weights, so no weight change fixes it. `JobSetting` carries the job's needs (`JOB_NEEDS` in `improveJob.ts`): PvE needs a propulsion module (afterburner, MWD or micro jump drive), Explore needs an MWD, PvP needs nothing (fleets split roles). Job weights are unchanged.
- **The Makeover adds it as a forced first step, not a constraint on the result.** When the open fit has no propulsion, `findMakeover` first applies the passing move that costs the least score (a medium module replaced, or the first free medium slot), then searches round it. A check on the finished fit would let the greedy spend the slot first and then fail the rule, or undo its own work to make room. If no move passes the Guardrails (for example the PvE damage limit), nothing is forced. The row says why ("PvE: adds propulsion").
- **Which kind: the hull's fits, then rules.** The kind most of the hull's fits for the job carry (`commonPropulsionKind`: group only, the engine's score picks the variant) leads; the rules follow it as the fallback: a hull that can fit a Large Micro Jump Drive (`pool.flyable`) gets one, because the engine has no figure for a jump, and any other hull an afterburner or MWD by score. Explore asks for an MWD whatever the data says.
- **Micro jump drives join the propulsion job (`1189`).** A fitted one satisfies the PvE need and is protected like propulsion. Because the engine scores no value for a jump, no swap ever turns a micro jump drive into another kind (`kindBlocks`), and under Explore an MWD never becomes an afterburner or a jump drive.
- **Opt-out.** "No propulsion needed", under PvE in Search settings, off by default, remembered on this device (`fittingsImproveNoPropulsion`), hydrated with the other Improve settings. Explore ignores it. A Makeover whose result no longer beats the best single swap because of the forced module is not shown (the single swaps still are).
- **An Explore fit that already has an afterburner is left alone.** The Makeover adds an MWD only when the fit has no propulsion at all; it does not turn a fitted afterburner into an MWD. A single swap may (afterburner to MWD is allowed), the score decides.
