# Scope decisions — Mining crystals get a guide by ore family

_Recorded 2026-10-03._

- **A mining laser's crystals are explained, not ranked.** Which crystal is right depends on the ore in front of the pilot and on what they want from the rock, neither of which the Fitting knows, so the guide has no quick picks and dims nothing. It says what Type A, B and C are for in a line each (A: most ore from each rock; B: most ore an hour, rocks run out sooner; C: clears rocks, little yield) and what Tech II adds, then groups the crystals by the ore family they are cut for, each family naming its ores, with that family's six crystals side by side: m³/s for the whole group with that crystal loaded (crits in), cycle, residue m³/s and hub price, from the same one-calculation-per-charge engine run as the other guides. The loaded crystal's family starts open; the rest open on their header. Show info stays on the row's menu. The A/B/C lines were checked against the pinned engine (Simple Asteroid II crystals on a Hulk), not written from memory.
- **The ores in each family are a hand table, and an unknown family names none.** A crystal's valid targets are an SDE type list (attribute 3148) this app's data doesn't carry, so `crystalChoice.ts` lists the ores of the asteroid, Mercoxit and moon families by name. Erratic Ore crystals and the newer ores (Equinox, Pochven, Prismaticite, X-Grade) are left unnamed rather than guessed; their crystals still show, under their own family.
