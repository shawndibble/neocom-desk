# Scope decisions — Player: one person's Characters, in two layers

_Recorded 2026-11-19._

- **A Player is its own object, separate from the Corp Tax Book Miner, so the Members list, the mining page and app sign-in all use it.** A Player need not mine: a primary who never mines can be grouped with the alts that do. The **Miner** becomes a corporation's billing view of a Player. Rules out growing the Miner into the Player (a Miner exists only once a character has mined) and a second, unrelated record that could disagree with it.
- **Two layers, so an alt link never reaches a corporation that was not given it.** The corp layer (`corps/{id}/…`) holds a corporation's own groupings and removals, readable by that corporation's officers and the Player's own characters. The global layer holds only **Self-proven links**, readable only by the Player's own characters; a corporation sees a link only for a character in its own book, computed server-side, and clients never query the global layer by an arbitrary character id. Rejected: one global Player holding officer claims (an officer in corp A would expose alt links to corp B).
- **A player groups their own characters by signing in as each.** The app sends each character's access token to a callable, which verifies it with the existing `verifyAccessToken` (no new EVE sign-in, no new scope) and stores the owner hash. A later owner-hash change drops the link, so a sold character does not stay with its old owner. A character whose login expired must sign in again to count as proven.
- **Officers add characters, inside or outside the corporation, with no owner confirmation.** This supersedes the guest-confirmation rule of `20261018-194700`. The privacy that confirmation guarded moves to reading rights: an **Officer claim** is billed with the group but is left out of the readers (`uids`) until its owner signs in as it, and the group's name, main and members are never shown to it before then. The added character may decline the link, and an officer may remove it; both write a removal for that corporation only, and a removed character is never re-added by sign-in (it is flagged to officers instead).
- **Mail goes to the Player's primary that is in the corporation.** Only when the Player has no character in the corporation, or the character is in no group, may it go to a character outside it. An outsider is never made the main of a group that has a corp member.
- **Sign-in groups automatically, and merging two Miners combines them.** Merging uses the existing regroup (#6450): the absorbed Miner's Late notices, payments, write-offs, declarations and disputes move onto the surviving Miner, so its balance is one running account and a later Late notice lists every unpaid day of the group. Only when a merge is too large for one transaction (over 450 writes) or two groups in one corporation claim the same character does an officer get a "merge these?" prompt instead; a character claimed twice is a conflict shown to an officer, never last-writer-wins. Groups in different corporations never conflict. A self-proven link outranks an officer claim only in the global layer.
- **Billing keeps the Miner id as its key.** A Player's own id is never a record key, so no Late notice, payment or dispute is re-keyed. Rejected: re-keying everything onto a Player id (a one-off migration of every book for no player-visible gain).
- **First slice:** the global Player and sign-in auto-group, the Members list "Group with…" (outside characters too) and its badge, the conflict record, and the decline button. A merge screen and billing by Player id come later.
- **Rollout is additive** (dev and prod share one Firebase): new collections and fields only; `pendingConfirmation` data, its callables and its index stay readable until old clients are gone. Every existing group is kept as it is, with the same members, main and records. Who can read it does not change: members who confirmed or are in the corporation keep their access, and one still waiting to confirm stays unreadable until they sign in. Officers can then add corp members who have not mined to a group.
