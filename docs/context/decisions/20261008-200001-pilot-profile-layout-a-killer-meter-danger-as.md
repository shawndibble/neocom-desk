# Scope decisions — Pilot profile layout A: killer meter, danger as a share, all-time line

_Recorded 2026-10-08._

- **The profile reads top to bottom as: a compact header, the Threat band, three meters, Where they kill, the ships row, the all-time line, then recent kills.** This finishes the "verdict first" layout whose first half is `20261008-181210`. The header is the portrait beside the name and badge, the corporation and alliance on one line, and a facts line (security status, age, zKillboard link); the old label/value table is gone.
- **The Threat band names the facts behind the verdict as chips.** The kind of space they hunt in (and any other space with a fifth or more of their recent kills), a gang chip at 70% gangs or more, "Mostly solo" at 30% or less, and a pod chip from a quarter of their kills. Chips are toned by what they say (nullsec and wormhole red, lowsec amber, the rest neutral) and always carry words. They are not clickable, so §6c's pill row covers them as well as the badge.
- **A third meter, Kills vs losses, ends Victim and Killer.** Its value is `killerRatio` in `zkillFigures.ts`: kills as a share of kills and losses by ship count, so 610 kills and 304 losses reads "67% killer". It is not zKillboard's danger ratio, which weights each ship by size (that is why the same pilot shows 42% danger), and it is not ISK efficiency. It replaces ISK efficiency as the "am I looking at a killer or a victim" signal, and it appears wherever the meters do, so the Corporation and Alliance tabs get it too.
- **The danger meter always reads "N% dangerous".** zKillboard's ratio is the dangerous share, so it is quoted as that at either end; "N% snuggly" is dropped. The Snuggly to Dangerous ends and the colours by side stay (`20261002-145207`, `20261002-163430`), so a 42% danger pilot reads "42% dangerous" in green, the low side of the scale.
- **The two ship lists sit side by side in `PilotShips`.** "Flies on kills" is zKillboard's all-time top ships; "Kills most" is the hulls in their latest 200 kills. The ranked bars under "Ships used most on kills" stay for the Corporation and Alliance tabs only.
- **A pilot's all-time figures are one line.** Kills (green), losses (red), ISK destroyed and lost, and solo kills, with the "as zKillboard states them" note under it. The six stat tiles stay on the Corporation and Alliance tabs.
