# Scope decisions — PI Plan Bigger chains: P3 and P4 recommended when the pilot opts in to hauling between planets (issue #2794)

_Recorded 2026-10-06 · issue #2794. Code: `src/features/pi/biggerChainsModel.ts`, `useBiggerChains.ts`, `BiggerChainsPanel.tsx`, `piSettings.ts`. Amends [20261005-123211](20261005-123211-pi-recommendation-model-stoptier-scorer-absolute-income-stalled.md) ("P3+ are never offered"), [20261005-114103](20261005-114103-pi-section-becomes-plan-map-and-colonies-tabs.md) (one-planet answers) and [20261006-095530](20261006-095530-pi-p3-and-p4-get-a-multi-planet.md) ("never a pick")._

- **Plan recommends a P3 or P4 only when the pilot says they will haul between their planets.** A new PI setting, "I will haul between my planets" (`piSettings.haulBetweenPlanets`), is off by default and stored only when on. It sits on Plan under "What matters more?" and in the shared PI settings form. Pilots live everywhere, nullsec included, so frequent hauls are never assumed: the setting is the opt-in. With it off nothing changes, and P3/P4 stay estimates only (All products, the Map's tiles and drawer).
- **With it on, Plan's "Make more" answer gains a separate Bigger chains section, below the rebuilds.** No figure in it enters a one-planet pick, a quick win, the headline, a total, Colonies or the Map. Decisions 20261005-123211 and 20261006-095530 still stand for everything outside this section.
- **Only chains the pilot's own planet types can make.** Every raw must come from a planet type they run, and one of those types must host every factory. Two layouts are tried, and each product shows the one that gains more. **On their colonies**: the Goal Planner's solver on their real colonies (measured rates, own Command Centers and customs). Its figure is what the colonies the chain uses earn under it, and a colony it leaves alone keeps its own pick. **On new planets** of their types: the chain estimate from 20261006-095530, only when the chain fits their free planet slots.
- **A chain is recommended only when it beats the same number of planets on their best one-planet picks.** On colonies, the comparison is the colonies it uses after their quick wins and rebuild (Plan's own figure). On new planets, it is a free slot each at the best one-planet recipe (the slot nudge's figure). A side with no figure is "can't compare", never a win. A chain that does not beat sits in a collapsed list under the recommendations, so the pilot still sees the facts. If none beats, the section says so.
- **The solver looks for the most the chain's planets make in full, not the best split.** It may draw in every colony that helps. A smaller chain beside a few one-planet picks could sometimes gain more. Weighing every subset was left out, because it multiplies the solver runs by the number of colony subsets.
- **Jumps between colonies are counted on the Route Safety basis, as the Goal Planner counts them, and passed to the solver.** The solver then prefers the nearer factory planet. A pair with no known route reads "unknown", never zero. New planets have no distance yet, and the card says so. Each card shows the haul as m³ a week and m³ a trip at the pilot's own haul cadence.
- **Priced lazily, and only while the setting is on.** The candidates (usually a handful, not all ~30) are priced one layout at a time between frames. The results are kept for the last set of assumptions.
