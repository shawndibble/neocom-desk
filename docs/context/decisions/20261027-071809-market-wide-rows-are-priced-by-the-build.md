# Scope decisions — Market-Wide rows are priced by the Build Plan engine, blueprint included

_Recorded 2026-10-27._

- **A Market-Wide row is priced by `resolveBuildPlan`, the call a Build Plan opened from it makes — not by a flattened-tree shortcut.** Replaces `computeMarketWideRows`. The shortcut priced materials plus the job fee and nothing else, so every row whose blueprint costs real ISK read as a ~94% margin (revenue less fees) while the plan it opened showed a loss. Each survivor of the liquidity cut becomes a one-run plan record at the row's ME/TE, the scanned hub and the remembered build location, and the row's profit, margin, ISK/hour, seconds and build cost are that plan's result. Supersedes "The price is not used" for contracts in `20260928-223018` and the "same figures except sub-builds" caveat in the glossary.
- **A row whose blueprint nothing can price is left out, never shown at zero.** The plan engine reads an unpriced blueprint as an unpriced material and the plan page shows "No price data yet" for it; the scan follows. This is the existing "known gap" of `20260929-094139` (an auction-only contract, a blueprint NPC-seeded outside the hub's region) made visible as fewer rows rather than a wrong margin. The Blueprint source filter still reads the obtainability sets, so a pilot who filters to Contract sees only contracts a plan can price.
- **Loaded once per scan.** One price snapshot over the union of every survivor plan's types, one blueprint-purchase-offers read, one contract snapshot, one invention read; resolving 2,000 plans is ~60 ms of arithmetic (measured on the real SDE). A troll sell order still prices the product at CCP's average, passed to the engine as the product's price.
- **Depth Prices and Community Offers are not carried.** Depth Prices walk an order book per material and Community Offers come from a page hook; the plan page applies both to the one plan it shows. A row can therefore read slightly kinder than the plan it opens when a material need is large, or pessimistic where a Community Member shares the copy free.
- **Rows are priced as the active Character**: its owned blueprints are free, the plan's owner is that Character, and an alt's LP still counts through the same account-wide offer read the plan uses.
