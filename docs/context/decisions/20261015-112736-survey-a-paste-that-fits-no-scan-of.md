# Scope decisions — Survey: a paste that fits no scan of the survey starts a new one without asking

_Recorded 2026-10-15._

- **A Survey Scan that does not fit even the Survey's first scan starts a new Survey with no question.** A scan that only shrinks the field is an update; a scan that grew past the latest scan but still fits the first one could be the same field mined and re-scanned, so the owner is still asked (Create new / Add to existing). One that fits neither is clearly another field, and asking only adds a click. Rules out silently adding it to the open Survey.
