# Scope decisions — Manifest is a sixth Trade Hub that every-hub views skip (issue #5823)

_Recorded 2026-11-01 · issue #5823._

- **Manifest is a pickable Trade Hub.** Manifest V - AIR Laboratories Trade Center (station 60015249, Exordium region 10001004) is the sixth entry in `TRADE_HUBS`, so every hub picker lists it: Settings > Market's default and per-character hubs, the Market Browser, Build Plan, PI, Appraisal and Hauling's own hub. Exordium is a PvP-free starter region whose one gate leads to Yulai; its hub Manifest holds about a quarter of the region's orders. A pilot who lives there asked for it. Where the full station name is shown it reads "Manifest V - AIR Laboratories Trade Center"; where only the system is shown it reads "Manifest" (`name` vs `systemName`, as for every hub).
- **Every-hub views stay at five.** Compare drawer's Trade hubs view, Appraisal's Compare hubs, Order detail's other-hub prices and Hauling's Any hub use `COMPARED_HUBS` (`TradeHub.compared`). A pilot who stays in Exordium is not shopping the empire hubs, and a sixth column buys little. Standing-buyback Requests and the Corp Tax Book's hub setting also stay at the five, because their Cloud Functions validate the hub ids.
- **Backend jobs follow in a second change.** The daily jobs (open prices, hauling books, LP-store volume) and the snapshot hub list still cover five regions, so Hauling offers Manifest only once they publish Exordium (`TradeHub.dailyJobs`, `DAILY_JOB_HUBS`). Until then a pilot whose hub is Manifest opens Hauling on Jita. Cloud Functions are deployed by hand.
- **Provisional Exordium fee.** Manifest carries a 5-point broker-fee surcharge (`brokerSurchargePct`), from the EVE University wiki's "additional 5% market fees added to non-instant orders". Whether that is broker fee, sales tax or both is unknown, and the industry installation fee rise is not applied. The surcharge reaches the broker fee, break-even and relist floor wherever the pilot's hub standing is carried; an open order's floor at Manifest uses the order's station standing, which has no surcharge yet. Real numbers are tracked in #5823; do not treat the figure as measured.
- **Region-wide pricing is a later option.** Letting a pilot pick any region as their market (priced region-wide) is a separate, larger change, since hub prices are looked up by station.
