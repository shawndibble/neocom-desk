# Scope decisions — Verify page leads with owed now and how to pay

_Recorded 2026-11-19._

- **The guest verify page is one column that leads with the figure owed and a Pay panel.** Owed now is one large figure; beside it the Pay panel holds To (the corporation's name alone), the Reason code and a Copy button on each, so a Miner can paste them into EVE's transfer window. The old right column of Terms prose is gone. What is left of Terms is one dim rate line under the mined days; the price basis already heads that table. The late notice's eyebrow reads "Late notice", not the old period title.
- **No due date on the page.** A late notice has no due date of its own and Overdue is an officer's measure, so the page prints none; `dueDate` is removed from `verifyView`. The mail's own due date is unchanged.
- **The Wallet line shows only when the book reads past the Master Wallet.** The corporation's transfer window offers a division to its own members; a donation from outside the corporation is understood to land in the Master Wallet, so a book reading only division 1 names no wallet. This is observed behaviour, not documented by CCP; revisit if a non-member can pick a division.
- **The guest header is the standard public one.** Logo mark and "Neocom Desk" linking home, in a centred `max-w-4xl` shell like Remove now and Community join. The "Read-only · no login needed" badge is dropped.
- **The page lists only the days still owed, and keeps all history in a closed Ledger.** "Still owed" is one line per day, oldest first, each opening to its ore, prices and tax; a day paid in full is gone and a part-paid one shows what remains with "of {tax}". The Ledger (closed until asked for) lists every mined day, including a day mined since the last notice, and every payment, newest first. It carries no running balance and no waivers or write-offs: the server cannot rebuild them without the officer client's prices, and a balance that disagreed with Owed now would be worse than none.
- **The officer's client records the days still owed.** `recordAccounts` gains `unpaidDays` (date, what remains, whole tax) from the running allocation; the server stores it on the Miner's account and `liveAccount` takes payments made since off the oldest day, as the book does. The read also returns `ledger` (each date once from the newest Late notice that holds it, plus the net payments since the book's start). Both fields are additive: a Miner whose account was recorded by an older client, or a server that has not been redeployed, gets the older page (every line of the notice and its payments). Cloud Functions are not auto-deployed, so the new page shows real days only once `recordCorpTaxBookEdit` and the read callable are redeployed and an officer has opened the book once.
- **A day mined after the notice was issued has no ore to show.** The notice's lines are what was unpaid at issue; such a day is a plain line, not an opening one.
