# Scope decisions — Refine-vs-sell values the leftover units, at the same price the sell side quotes (issue #1048)

_Recorded 2026-09-14 · issue #1048._

- **The refine side of the highlight is what the player ends up holding: the whole batches refined, plus the leftover units sold.** A part batch refines into nothing, so comparing the refine total against the full pasted quantity charged refining for units it never consumed. Measured on live Jita buy orders, that recommended the worse option on 799 (type, quantity) pairs of ordinary ore, the worst by 1,036,890 ISK. This rules out reading the two displayed totals against each other as though they covered the same goods.
- **The leftover is valued at the buy price, because that is the assumption the sell side already makes for every unit.** The ticket flagged the conservative alternative — suppress the highlight when the leftover has no price — as a judgement call. The two rules agree on every row the comparison itself answers: `buyEach` prices both the sell side and the leftover, so a row that cannot value one cannot value the other, and `refineBeatsSellAsIs` answers false there rather than treating the leftover as free. They differ only on what the panel does with that false: it reads "no comparison" as "selling won" and bolds the sell-as-is cell, which on such a row is a dash. That is unchanged from before this fix and deliberately left alone — marking a winner where no prices exist is a separate defect from the one measured here, and it decides no ISK.
- **A tie reads as sell, never as refine.** Below one whole batch the leftover is the entire paste and both paths come to exactly the same ISK. The comparison stays strictly greater-than.
- **No displayed figure changes.** The refine total and the sell total are both honest already; only which cell is marked the winner was wrong. This rules out folding the leftover into the refine total, which would misreport what refining yields.
- **The comparison lives in `engine/market/appraisal.ts`, beside the row shape it reads**, rather than in the panel — it is arithmetic that decides advice, so it is unit-tested. It stays a function, not a field on the row: a field would invite the CSV export and the shareable link to carry a winner, both of which are out of this ticket's scope.
