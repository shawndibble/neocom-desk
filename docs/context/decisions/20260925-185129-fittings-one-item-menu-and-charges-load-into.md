# Scope decisions — Fittings: one item menu, and charges load into every module that takes them

_Recorded 2026-09-25._

- **A charge loads into every fitted module that takes it, whatever the module's type.** "Takes it" is the module's own charge groups plus the engine's size and capacity check (`checkCharges`) — one rule for ammo, missiles, bombs, scripts, crystals, cap boosters and paste. It replaces loading by module type, so a Heavy and a Heavy Assault launcher that fire the same missile both load from one pick. Alt on a drop, or "Load into…", loads just the one module.
- **Loading out of Cargo spends the cargo; loading from the Add panel doesn't.** From cargo each module takes a full load (its charge capacity over the charge's volume) while there is one and the remainder when there isn't — a part-load counts as loaded — and the charge it held goes back into the hold, as many as the app took out of that hold for it (a part-load gives back its part) — whether it is replaced by a charge from cargo, from the Add panel or a dropdown, or simply unloaded. A charge that didn't come out of the hold (the Add panel, a Load, a Share Link) gives nothing back: the Fitting doesn't know how many the module held. The count is kept only while the Fitting stays open; no link or export carries it, so a reopened Fitting's charges give nothing back either. A shortfall says so: "loaded 3 of 4 — cargo ran out". The Add panel's Charges tab and a module's own "Load … into all compatible" leave the hold alone, as before.
- **The cargo hold is shown against its fitted capacity but never capped.** Unlike the drone bay, adding cargo or getting a charge back can take it over; the bar turns red and flashes once, as the drone bay's does. Any market item can be added to cargo, from the Add panel's Cargo tab.
- **One item menu for the whole editor** (`FittingItemMenu`, on the app's `RowActionsMenu`): Ring tiles, empty slots, List rows, drones, cargo and Add panel items, plus the Start screen / My Fittings / In-game rows, Compare columns, and an Assets ship's "Open in Fittings". It opens on right-click, touch-and-hold, or a ⋮ button where the row has room, so touch and keyboard reach what the mouse does; a Ring tile's tooltip gives up touch-and-hold to it. Every drag has a menu equivalent (Load into…, Move up / down / to slot, Launch all).
- **Drag stays pointer-only**, now on the List as well as the Ring: charges from Cargo or the Charges tab, Add panel modules onto a rack, List rows by their grip, bay drones onto Drones to launch them.
- **Compare has no baseline column**, so a column's menu is Open in the editor and Remove.
