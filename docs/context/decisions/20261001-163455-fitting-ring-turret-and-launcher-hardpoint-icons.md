# Scope decisions — Fitting Ring: turret and launcher hardpoint icons

_Recorded 2026-10-01._

- **Each kind of hardpoint pip on the Ring's rim is headed by the game's own hardpoint icon: turret just left of 12 o'clock, launcher just right.** Before this, the two runs of pips were identical circles told apart only by side or a hover, so a split hull (a Typhoon, a Raven Navy Issue) showed two anonymous rows of dots. The glyph shows in the compact phone overview too, which has no tooltip. To make room the pips start a little further out and sit a little closer together (`hardpointPipAngles`). Eight of each still fit between the calibration and drone bandwidth bands.
- **A high-slot tile whose module takes a hardpoint carries the same icon as a corner badge (top-left; the cantUse flag keeps top-right and the charge bottom-right), and its tooltip says "Uses a turret/launcher hardpoint".** This connects each pip to the module using it. A utility high (a neut, a cloak) takes no hardpoint and gets no badge, which also shows why a hull's pips can add up to fewer than its high slots. No badge is drawn until the type's kind is known (`useFittingHardpoints().kindOf`).
- **The icons are CCP's art, sourced the same way as the Add panel's slot icons** (`20260930-173310-fitting-add-panel-slot-icon-replaces-fits-this.md`). They are copied from the EVE University wiki (`Icon_turret_hp.png`, `Icon_launcher_hp.png`, 64×64) into `public/images/fitting/hardpoint-{turret,launcher}.png` and credited under the Data credit. DESIGN.md §5's raster exception now names them. Phosphor has no turret glyph, and a drawn one would look different from what pilots know from the client.
