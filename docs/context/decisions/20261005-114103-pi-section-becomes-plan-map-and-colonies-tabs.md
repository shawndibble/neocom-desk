# Scope decisions — PI section becomes Plan, Map and Colonies tabs; Advisor retires

_Recorded 2026-10-05. Spec, mockups and reference shots: [`docs/design/pi-tabs/`](../../design/pi-tabs/README.md)._

- **Plan is the landing tab, Map is the explorer, and Colonies is the daily check.** Plan is a question-first coach. It opens on "Make more from my planets" when the pilot has colonies and on "Find the best thing to build" when they have none. It answers in one plain sentence per planet, then gives an in-game checklist. Map is the visual explorer: planet types against the product tiers, pre-marked from the pilot's colonies, with what-if planet types. Colonies is what a pilot opens every day: what needs doing, and by when. The goal planner answered the rarest question first, and a new-player panel froze on a map-first landing.
- **The Advisor is split three ways, and the tab retires.** Its optimisation half (what each planet should make) moves to Plan. Its fault-finding half (stopped extractors, idle factories, full storage) moves to Colonies. Its unbuilt-planet cards ("where to put a new colony") move to the Map. No PI surface keeps the name Advisor.
- **Quick wins come first and add to today's income; rebuilds are the alternative.** A **Quick win** is in-place tuning of a colony as it stands. Plan lists every one before any rebuild, and quotes a rebuild's gain on top of the quick wins, never instead of them. This keeps ADR 0012's tuning-vs-rebuild split: tuning works from today's measured extraction, and changing what a colony extracts is a separate, opt-in change list. The Goal Plan from decision [20261004-231402](20261004-231402-pi-plan-tab-becomes-a-goal-planner-measured.md) survives as Plan's "Make a specific product".
- **Colonies gains a "Needs a look" status.** A colony needs a look when its storage or launchpad fills before the pilot's next haul, or when it has idle factories. Neither stops income today, but both lose income before the next visit.
- **Recommendations never suggest selling raw P0.** Every recommendation ends at P1 or above. Extracted P0 is never priced at zero either: a colony that changes product gives up its current income, and the comparison with today counts that loss.
- **Every ISK figure is ISK/day after customs, at the market the pilot sells to, compared with today.** That market is a trade hub, or the pilot's corp buyback at its percentage. One unit everywhere means no gross margins and no per-hour or per-unit figures in recommendations.
- **This supersedes issues #2621 and #2622, and #2618 becomes "retire the Advisor".** The Advisor and Plan-tab refinements those issues asked for are covered by the three tabs.
- **The planet finder reads a static system → planet-type file plus the local stargate graph.** The file is derived from the SDE. Together with the stargate graph that `src/engine/route/` already uses, it answers "nearby systems with a Lava planet" without an ESI call per system, which would cost hundreds of requests for one search.
