# Scope decisions — Depletion by ore ends with the pace-by-ore chart

_Recorded 2026-10-24. Amends [Survey chart views swap the whole chart area](20261017-145310-survey-chart-views-swap-the-whole-chart-area.md)._

- **The Depletion by ore view is two charts: depletion on top, the mining rate stacked by ore (m³/s) under it.** Reading the per-ore rate belongs under the eight ore lines it explains. The earlier decision's "every other view is one chart" and "mining rate by ore not shipped" no longer hold for this view, so it is a little taller than the ISK view. The pace chart shares the depletion chart's time axis and sits in the same `h-32` slot as Ore left's.
- **Ore left keeps the plain Total pace chart, and there is no Total / By ore toggle anywhere.** Each view shows one fixed pace chart; nothing to remember or re-choose when switching views.
- **A Survey with one scan or fewer draws no pace chart in either view,** as before. ISK left is unchanged: one chart.
