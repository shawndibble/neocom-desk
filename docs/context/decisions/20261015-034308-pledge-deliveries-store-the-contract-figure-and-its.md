# Scope decisions — Pledge deliveries store the contract figure and its finish time

_Recorded 2026-10-15._

- **A Pledge line stores `auto` (what contracts alone account for) and `through` (the latest finish time counted into it), beside `delivered`, `contractIds` and `manual`.** The figure was being rebuilt from a capped id list, which double counted or lost deliveries once contracts aged out of ESI, a typed amount hid the contract figure, or a late contract carried a lower id than a full list. A visible contract is already counted when its id is recorded or it finished by `through`; `delivered` is the larger of `auto` and `manual`, never their sum; new `auto` is the old plus newly counted contracts. `auto` is kept unclamped so lowering then raising a Pledge restores it. Rules out rebuilding delivery from the id list; entries without the fields still read (auto from `delivered`, ids only).
- **`contractIds` is capped at 10 a line (was 20), as evidence only.** The rules' 1000-expression budget is the binding limit and the two new fields cost about 20 more a line; ten ids keep a full line writable alone. Older lines with more ids are trimmed when next rewritten.
- **A contract counts toward one Pledge.** A contract another of the Member's Pledges already records is not credited to a Pledge that does not (oldest Request first for new ones), so pledging on an older Request later never counts a delivery twice.
- **An unread contract holds back everything finished after it.** Contracts are read oldest first; one whose items could not be read (a failed or capped read) means nothing finishing at or after it is counted this run, so `through` never passes a contract that was not looked at and nothing is counted twice. A contract is given up on only for a definite answer about it alone (ESI 404; a 403 is a revoked Permission and asks for the grant instead), at least twice over at least five minutes in a session, with a successful read clearing the count; offline, rate limits and 5xx never count, so a flaky network cannot lose a delivery. A given-up contract is skipped for good (never counted, `through` advances past it) so it cannot pin the rest. After a reload it is tried again, but if `through` has already passed it, it stays skipped. Contracts every Pledge has already counted are not read at all, and only reads that went to ESI count against the 40-reads-per-run cap, so a first sync with many contracts finishes over a few runs.
- **One contract, one Pledge, by item.** Another Pledge for the same requester that already took the same item of a contract (its id is recorded, or it counted through that contract's finish time) blocks this Pledge from crediting it; a different item of the same contract is still credited to the line that asks for it.
- **Known limits, accepted.** Two contracts finishing in the same second as `through` can undercount one of them. A legacy entry with no `through` and a full old 20-id list recounts its trimmed contracts once on the first run. A contract split across a lowered Pledge does not credit its remainder when the Pledge is raised again; the Member can type an amount. `auto` is bounded at 10^12 and `through` at 4*10^12 in the rules.
- **More accepted limits.** A contract partly taken by a small Pledge (a 30-unit contract against a 10-quantity Pledge) does not credit its remainder to a later Pledge: the slot had no room for it, and the typed amount recovers it. Two contracts finishing in the same second as `through` can undercount one; this is untested.
