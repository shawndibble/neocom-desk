# Scope decisions — Settings sections rail and log out of this device

_Recorded 2026-09-25._

- **Settings has no tab bar; it has a grouped rail of sections beside the app rail.** Groups: App (Display, Mobile tabs, Shortcuts, Permissions), Defaults (Industry, Market, Characters, Corporation), Alerts (Notifications), Data & device (Data & storage, This device, Activity Log, FAQ & credits). Before, General was one long scroll and its Defaults panel held every synced default in one column. A phone gets one grouped select above the section instead of a second column. A list-then-drill phone layout was mocked up and dropped, because it needs an index route that the shared tabbed-page machinery does not have. Each section is still a path segment, so `notifications`, `dataAge`, `activity` and `faq` keep their URLs.
- **The old General anchors still resolve.** `/settings/general` is no longer a section, so `TabRoute` sends it to Display; a `#shortcuts` or `#corp-access` hash on it is then carried on to Shortcuts or Permissions.
- **Planetary Industry's expiring window sits under Industry, in a panel of its own.** It was the one Defaults control with no obvious home. Corporation stays hidden from the rail unless the active Character can reach a corporation, and its URL says so instead of showing an empty page.
- **The cache clear, the reset of remembered views and the CCP data credit moved out of General.** The first two are in Data & storage, the credit is in FAQ & credits.
- **"Log out of this device" logs out of every Character on this browser and touches nothing remote.** It is `removeCharacter` over the whole roster with the remote purge off, so a Character's local rows, cached game data, notifications and tokens go, while the synced defaults and the sync configuration in `db.settings` stay. Logging a Character back in pulls their plans again. That is the difference from removing one Character on the Characters page, which also deletes the Character's synced data. Where sync is configured, it first tries one push per Character (eight seconds at most each), so an edit waiting on its debounce is not lost. Where it is not, the panel says plans stored only here are deleted. With no Character left the app goes to the login page on its own.
