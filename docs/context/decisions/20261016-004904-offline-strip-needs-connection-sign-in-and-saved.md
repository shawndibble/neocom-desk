# Scope decisions — Offline strip, needs-connection sign-in and saved data under a lapsed grant

_Recorded 2026-10-16._

- **One slim offline strip in the shell, at every width, in flow.** It sits at the top of `<main>`, never fixed, so it covers no content and needs no safe-area or tab-bar handling. Neutral, not a warning: the saved data below is still good. It replaces the rail's sync dot as the only offline signal a phone has; the dot stays.
- **Every control that starts EVE's SSO is disabled offline** (`aria-disabled` plus a "Needs a connection" tooltip, via `NeedsConnection`). Offline, that navigation strands an installed app on the browser's error page. `ReauthBanner` and `EveSsoButton` carry it themselves. The command palette's "Add Character" is not a button and is not guarded, and `loginFlow` has no `isOnline()` gate: `navigator.onLine` stays advisory, used for UI only (W1). `NeedsConnection` renders the same tree online and offline (`Tooltip` with empty content renders just the trigger), so a connectivity change never remounts the control or drops focus.
- **A lapsed grant over saved data shows the data with a small "Log in again" note**, not the full banner in its place. The full banner stays when there is nothing saved. Skills, Clones, Assets Items, Mail and Contacts do this; on the Overview, a widget opts in per kind (`hasStaleData`), and a kind has to confirm its saved payload actually holds the figures or it would show a blank "—" under a note. Wallet, Training, Mail, Coming up, Contracts and Orders opt in. Mining stays out: its `needsReauth` aggregates across Characters while the note targets the active one.
- **`DataAgeBadge` stays hidden below `md`.** The existing decision (data age off phone headers) stands, and the cached-data notice with its age on every width is the planned phone answer (the `CachedNotice` primitive), not a compact badge crowding the header.
- **`components/ui` primitives may use `useTranslation` for their own shared `common.*` strings, and `NeedsConnection` may read the network store.** `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` said no primitive imports i18next, which many already did. `NeedsConnection` is the first to read a store (`useOnline`); that is deliberate, since its purpose is to be a drop-in guard around any login control.
