# Scope decisions — A day closed within the tolerance stays closed: payments settle in the order paid (issue #6780)

_Recorded 2026-11-25 · issue #6780._

- **A Running account settles its payments one at a time, in the order paid.** Each payment pays the unpaid days dated before its own UTC day, oldest first. Those are the days billed by the time it arrived. The rest becomes credit. Credit pays each later day as soon as that day is billed, before the next payment. This replaces pouring every payment into one pool. With the pool, the next payment filled a day the tolerance had already closed, so the "Shortfall forgiven" row and the miner's tolerance Record Change (#6770) quietly stopped being true. Paying the amount the account said was owed then left the newest day short.
- **A day closed within the tolerance is final.** Once money leaves a day short by no more than the book's per-day tolerance (0.5% by default, or the book's own setting or the day's exemption), the day is closed and its gap is written off for good. Later money and credit skip that day and go to newer days or to credit. A payment short by more than the tolerance still leaves the rest owed, and the next money pays that day first.
- **A payment made on the day it mined waits until that day is billed.** It is credit until then. If it comes up short of the day's close-of-day tax by no more than the tolerance, that day closes as a market adjustment, and the next payment goes to the next day.
- **Settlement follows the payments' dates.** A payment recorded later with an earlier date, such as a backdated hand-recorded payment or a late-synced donation, settles in date order. It can therefore change which day after it closed within the tolerance. That is the same rule applied to the corrected history, as with any other correction to the record.
- **Everything else is unchanged:** corrections apply before any payment; officer write-offs apply after every payment; officer charges are never forgiven; held days, the stored Paid through, exemptions and checkpoints work as before.
- **A checkpoint plus the later days now reads exactly as the whole history.** That includes books with a tolerance. The randomised tolerance checkpoint test used to allow a difference of up to the amount forgiven, because the pool could fill again a gap the checkpoint had closed. It now asserts the same figures.
- **The server's live account follows the same rule.** `liveRunningAccount` (functions) feeds the Late notice's owed figure, the account-close statement and the disputes an officer write-off closes. It now applies each payment since the last record in turn to the recorded unpaid days. Before, it pooled them. Its `owed` still subtracts money without the tolerance, as before. `checkpointProblems` checks only the checkpoint's shape and is unchanged.
- **One-time effect on existing books.** Days after the last checkpoint are settled again under this rule. A past day the pool used to fill may now show a tolerance write-off, and the next sync writes its tolerance Record Change.
