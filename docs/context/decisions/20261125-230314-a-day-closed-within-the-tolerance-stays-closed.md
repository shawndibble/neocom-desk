# Scope decisions — A day closed within the tolerance stays closed: payments settle in the order paid (issue #6780)

_Recorded 2026-11-25 · issue #6780._

- **A Running account settles its payments one at a time, in the order paid.** Each payment pays the unpaid days billed by its UTC day, oldest first, and the rest becomes credit. Credit pays each later day as soon as that day is billed, before the next payment. It replaces pouring every payment into one pool. With the pool, the next payment filled a day the tolerance had already closed, so the "Shortfall forgiven" row and the miner's tolerance Record Change (#6770) quietly stopped being true. Paying the amount the account said was owed then left the newest day short.
- **A day closed within the tolerance is final.** Once money leaves a day short by no more than the book's per-day tolerance (0.5% by default, or the book's own setting or the day's exemption), the day is closed and its gap is written off for good. Later money and credit skip that day and go to newer days or to credit. A payment short by more than the tolerance still leaves the rest owed, and the next money pays that day first.
- **Everything else is unchanged:** corrections apply before any payment; officer write-offs apply after every payment; officer charges are never forgiven; held days, the stored Paid through, exemptions and checkpoints work as before.
- **A checkpoint plus the later days now reads exactly as the whole history.** That includes books with a tolerance. The randomised tolerance checkpoint test used to allow a difference of up to the amount forgiven, because the pool could fill again a gap the checkpoint had closed. It now asserts the same figures.
- **Server side unchanged.** `liveRunningAccount` (functions) only decides which disputes an officer write-off closes. It already starts from the recorded unpaid days, which leave out closed days. `checkpointProblems` checks only the checkpoint's shape. No Cloud Functions deploy is needed.
