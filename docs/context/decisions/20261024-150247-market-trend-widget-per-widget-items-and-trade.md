# Scope decisions — Market Trend widget: per-widget items and Trade Hubs, a year of range, and a hook feed (issue #5002)

_Recorded 2026-10-24 · issue #5002._

- **Each widget names its own items, or follows the Quickbar.** `trendItems` holds up to six type ids; absent means the active Character's Quickbar. The Items dialog picks from the Quickbar, the Minerals, the Ice products, one raw Ore per ore, and PLEX with the skill injectors, read from the market catalogue (market groups 1857, 1033, the children of 54, and fixed ids 44992 / 40520 / 45635). There is no free-text search: the lists are the presets, and the Quickbar covers anything else.
- **Each widget names its own Trade Hubs, or follows the Market hub.** `trendHubs` holds up to three hubs; absent means the pilot's Market hub (`useMarketHub`, which already honours the legacy key). A widget draws items × hubs, capped at six lines. An item with a global market (PLEX) is read at its own region and drawn once whatever the hubs.
- **The Overview's feed is now a hook source for this kind.** Feeds were per kind and per Character, so a widget's own picks could not reach a snapshot loader. `MarketTrendSource` reads the layout, resolves every Market Trend widget's lines, and reads each distinct line once through Price History's cached loader; a builder then picks its widget's lines out of the shared table. A change of picks reloads; a resize does not.
- **The range gains a year, for this kind only.** `WidgetRange` includes 365 and a kind opts in with `longRange`; every other ranged kind still offers 7, 30 and 90, and a stored 365 on one of them is reset on load. ESI keeps about 13 months of market history, so 400 days of rows are kept per line.
- **Daily averages only, indexed to 0%.** Unchanged from the first cut: ESI's `average`, each line indexed to its first known value so a 5 ISK mineral and a 5M ISK PLEX share a scale; the 7 and 30 day changes compare the newest row with the last row on or before that many days earlier. An ISK-axis toggle and a per-item volume overlay stay out.
- **The chart is its own inline-SVG `lines` kind**, not the Price History chart: that one is Recharts, which the Overview never imports.
