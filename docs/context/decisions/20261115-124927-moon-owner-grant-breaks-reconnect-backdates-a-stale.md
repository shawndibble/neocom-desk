# Scope decisions — Moon owner grant breaks: reconnect backdates, a stale mail once, own-member rate (issue #6210)

_Recorded 2026-11-15 · issue #6210._

- **A disconnected moon owner that reconnects is covered again from the day after its last covered day, no further back than 30 days and never before the day it last joined** (`reconnectCoverageFrom`, mirrored; the join floor matters when a spell never read well left no period, so the last one predates a leave). EVE still holds that ledger, so the first pull reads the ore of the gap and none of it is lost. A moon owner that was never covered at all starts today. Taking a disconnected moon owner back by a new invite link that asks no start day is the same reconnect; a link that names a day keeps it, clamped as before. Ore older than 30 days stays lost.
- **The stale banner counts down to the loss** (`daysUntilOreLost` / `oreLostOn`): the first unread day (the day after the last fully read one; never read well, the day it went stale) is dropped by EVE 31 days later. At 0 the banner says the oldest unread mining can never be counted.
- **A stale ledger is mailed once per stale spell, to both sides** (`grant-stale` mail kind): the moon owner's CEO and payee and the book's officers. Mail is client-only, so the first editor of the book whose client sees the moon owner unreadable (with the mail permission) claims the spell through `claimCorpTaxBookFeederStaleMailCallable` (a corpTaxBook router action), which writes `staleMailedFor` (the spell's `staleSince`) on the feeder record; every other officer is named nobody. A new spell (reconnected, then refused again) mails again. One of the book's own corporations is not mailed, like its other moon owner mail (the server refuses that claim too). Nor is a moon owner whose oldest unread day EVE already dropped: the mail's "reconnect before" date would be past (so moon owners disconnected long before this shipped are not mailed). It goes out when an editor next opens the book, not at the moment the ledger is refused, since no server holds a mail token. A claim whose queueing then fails is not retried.
- **A held day names the moon owner it waits for** (`DayRow.heldBy`): a `not-billable` day inside the #6209 hold reads "Waiting for {owner}'s ledger" instead of "Today's mining". Its status, and what can be done with it, is unchanged.
- **A removal whose notice mail has not gone out is its own status, `removal-unsent` ("Removal waiting on its notice mail"), and counts in "Need a look".** It never takes effect until the notice is recorded (`20261020-194451`); the strip's "I sent the notice" still records one sent by hand.
- **A book can set a miner rate for a moon owner's own members at its refineries** (`feeder-member`, `{ feederId }`). It applies only when the miner's recorded corporation is that moon owner, ranks above the per-moon-owner rate and the Guest default and below per-refinery, per-moon, per-Miner and per-record rates. With none set, those members are Guests as before. A client from before ignores the scope (it is not in its order), so a book that sets one reads it as before on an old client until it updates.
- **The moon owner's page lists its mining per day, with a Month filter, and says how the balance adds up** ("{accrued} has built up for you at your rate, less {paid} paid to you", from the summary). Days still carry ore and units only: per-day value, rate and due stay hidden (`20261026-160939`), so the accrued figure is the summary's total, not a per-month sum.
