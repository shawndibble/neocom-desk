# Scope decisions — Improve: free slots, Reach, Defense Choice and buy/refit modes (issue #5026)

_Recorded 2026-10-24 · issue #5026._

- **The pruning table is `src/engine/fittings/improveGroups.ts`.** Rework and the empty-slot fills try only inventory groups that can move the goal's stat: More damage, Best overall: Ballistic Control, Gyrostabilizer, Magnetic Field Stabilizer, Heat Sink, Drone Damage Amplifier, Missile Guidance Computer and the weapon rigs; Longer reach: Tracking Enhancer, Tracking Computer, Sensor Booster, ambit and locus rigs; Faster: Afterburner and Microwarpdrive, Inertial Stabilizers, Nanofiber, Overdrive Injector, Hyperspatial rigs; Tankier: the tank groups of the chosen side plus Damage Control and Reinforced Bulkheads. Cheaper has no groups, so its Rework is Rebalance. Weapons are never a target outside their own group, because a fit decides its hardpoints. A good idea outside a listed group is missed by design; adding the group id to the table is the fix.
- **Per-search caps keep the worker honest.** At most 20 targets per fitted module type, 40 fills per rack, 400 candidates per search, and the Make it fit pair cap (`PAIR_LIMIT`, 40) for pair moves. Candidates are ordered Upgrade, Rebalance, Rework, fills, so a capped search keeps the safest ones.
- **A fill takes the first empty slot of a rack.** Pilots apply one and search again; filling every empty slot with copies of one module would suggest eight of a module no hardpoint or grid takes. Weapons are never a fill.
- **Pair moves reuse Make it fit's estimate, not its loop.** An add that gains on the goal but breaks CPU or powergrid is a seed (best five). Each swap already calculated for the search is a possible downgrade; a pair is tried only when the swap frees at least the seed's overage on every resource, ranked by freed resources, the best 40 recalculated whole. No extra single-swap calculations are made, so pairs cost at most 40 more.
- **A Defense Choice frees the other side's tank modules.** Under Shield the armor plates, repairers, hardeners and armor rigs may be replaced by shield modules; under Armor the shield ones by armor. Damage Control and bulkheads are common to both and stay on Reach's rules. Non-tank modules never swap into tank modules while a side is chosen. Armor sits in low slots and shield in mid slots, so a hull whose lows are full of non-tank modules gets an empty Armor result, and the drawer says "no gain" for that side rather than hiding it. Keep as is searches only the side the fit already has (both when it has none or an even split). For a tank goal (Tankier, Best overall) a chosen Shield or Armor runs both sides, the chosen one first, and the drawer shows both best gains on one line.
- **Reach, mode and spend cap are remembered; Defense, Rigs and Officer and Deadspace are not.** They describe this fit, not the pilot's habits.
- **Money.** Planning to buy: a swap costs new minus old at the hub sell price (the Price section's `sellMin`), an add costs the full price, and the total budget is checked against the Price section's own total plus the change. Refitting: the same per-swap figure, except a rig change costs the full new rig, gets nothing back for the old one, and is flagged as destroyed. The cap is on the extra spend. A swap over the budget or cap is not offered and is listed under the closest swaps as "Costs more than your budget or spend cap". An unpriced option is never dropped for the budget and sorts last.
- **Rigs and Officer and Deadspace sit behind a collapsed "Also try…" group, both unticked.** Rigs were searched by the foundation ticket; they are now opt-in, as the ticket asks.
- **Guards that stay out of the table.** A second Damage Control or cloak is never offered. Rework never removes the last module doing a job the goal does not score (propulsion, scram or disruptor, web, EWAR, capacitor, remote repair, miners, cloak); Faster scores propulsion and Best overall scores capacitor, so those may move. The check runs per suggestion: ticking two suggestions that each remove one of two scrams still drops both, a known gap.
- **Browser timing is not yet measured for Rework.** The #5025 figure (30–55 ms a swap in the worker) still holds per swap; at the 400-candidate cap that is 12–22 s, so "Show best so far" after 10 s is the expected path on big fits, and Defense Choice on a tank goal doubles it. A timing run needs a pilot with trained skills (an unskilled e2e Character has every candidate under Train), so it stays open on #5026.
