# Scope decisions — Invention is a Blueprint Acquisition source, priced at expected cost (issue #3990)

_Recorded 2026-10-16 · issue #3990. Reverses `.out-of-scope/invention-planning.md` (deleted), with the owner's approval the same day._

- **Invention is a way to get a Tech II blueprint copy, not a plan of its own.** The out-of-scope entry's objections no longer hold: the invented copy needs no market price because here it is an input cost, and recursive sub-builds now carry a component's cost into its parent plan. A Build Plan is still one blueprint; invention only prices the copy that plan builds with. Scheduling copy or invention jobs, research (ME/TE) planning, and Tech III / reverse engineering stay out.
- **Expected cost per copy = attempt cost ÷ success chance; per run, ÷ the copy's runs.** One attempt is the datacores, the decryptor (if any), a 1-run T1 copy job and the invention job fee. The chance is the SDE base × (1 + Σscience/30 + encryption/40) × the decryptor multiplier, capped at 1, from the plan Character's trained levels. Every figure is labelled "expected": luck moves the real cost either way.
- **Each decryptor choice is its own tier.** "No decryptor" and every generic decryptor become separate offers (ME 2 + decryptor ME, TE 4 + decryptor TE, base runs + decryptor runs), so the existing cheapest-tier pick is the decryptor optimiser and the pilot overrides it like any other row. Invention competes on total cost with owned, contract, market and LP Store sources; a cheaper source at the same tier wins.
- **The copy job needs a T1 original the plan can see** (personal, or corp with Corp Assets on). Without one the Invent section says so and no invention tier is offered — a T1 copy is not assumed.
- **Fees are charged where the plan's own job runs.** Copy and invention jobs use the build system's copying and invention cost indices (read off the same ESI response as manufacturing), the plan's facility and tax, and 2% of one run's EIV of what the blueprint builds (the T1 product for the copy, the T2 product for invention). Datacores and decryptors are priced at the plan's Trade Hub, not the modal's hub picker.
- **Market-Wide counts invention as a blueprint source**, last in preference, for a product whose blueprint some Character can invent from an owned T1 original — the same rule the modal prices by, so a ranked row always prices when opened.
