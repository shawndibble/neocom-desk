# Scope decisions — Amarr VIII has a temporary 50% fee discount, ending at an assumed date

_Recorded 2026-11-21._

- **Amarr VIII's sales tax and broker fee are both halved.** Fractured Skies (live 2026-11-10 11:00 UTC) set up a "Deaconry of Economic Oversight" at Amarr VIII (Oris) - Emperor Family Academy, a Cradle of War campaign outcome, reported as a 50% cut for six months. It is that one station, not market-wide. Measured in game 2026-11-21: sales tax 1.69% at Accounting V (half of 3.375%), the broker fee halved, and the 100 ISK minimum broker fee unchanged. So the factor multiplies the rate after skills and standings (`TradeHub.feeDiscount`, `hubFeeDiscountFactor`, `fees.ts`'s trailing `discountFactor`). It never scales the 100 ISK minimum or a Safety Tax.
- **The end date is an assumption.** No source gives an end date. `AMARR_FEE_DISCOUNT_UNTIL_MS` is 2027-05-11 11:00 UTC, taken as the next expansion about six months after Fractured Skies. After it the discount stops on its own. It is one constant in `src/market/hubs.ts`: correct it when CCP announces the real date. A wrong guess errs toward standard (higher) fees.
- **Where it applies.** Everything that already carries the hub's `ResolvedStandings` (Appraisal, Hauling, Market order fees, build-vs-buy, realized profit, LP offers, plan verdicts, buy-order material prices) reads `standing.feeDiscountFactor`, filled beside `safetyTaxPct` when the standings resolve. The factor is read when standings resolve (page load), so a session left open across the end date keeps the discount until its next reload. Callers with no hub (Order Floor and order detail's relist floor, PI and ore estimates, the standings brokerage comparison, the hauling "instant" rough-fee constant) and player structures keep standard rates.
- **Relists are unmeasured.** A relist at Amarr is assumed to apply the Relist Discount to the already-halved broker rate (`relistBrokerFeePct`'s `discountFactor`). Not read in game.
- **Breakdown dialogs say so.** The Build Plan and Realized Profit breakdowns show a line when a discount applies, so a lower rate is never silent.
