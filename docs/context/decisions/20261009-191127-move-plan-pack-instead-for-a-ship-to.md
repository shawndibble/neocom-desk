# Scope decisions — Move plan: Pack instead for a ship to fly

_Recorded 2026-10-09._

- **A ship the Move plan says to fly can be packed instead.** The plan then hauls it as cargo at its packaged volume and re-plans trips. The trip count covers cargo only; a flown ship is its own flight and is never one of the hauler's trips.
- **Only a ship with fitted rigs asks first.** EVE repackages a ship only with nothing fitted, and unfitting a rig destroys it, so the warning appears when the assets show rigs on the ship, with a "Don't remind me again" checkbox kept as a device-local setting. A ship with no rigs packs without a prompt.
