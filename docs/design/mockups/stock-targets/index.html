Market › Stock: revised mockup

This replaces the four first-draft layouts. A Stock List now belongs to a market: a station or structure the pilot picks, where they sell. Listed counts their own sell orders at that market. In hangar counts their items sitting at the same station, ready to put up for sale. Status and Short are measured against what is listed. When there is more than one market, the tab opens on a list of markets, worst first, so the one that needs restocking is on top. Sample data only.
