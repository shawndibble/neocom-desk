Stock Targets: four layouts

First-draft mockups, sample data only. Scenario: a corp is opening a market in a local structure (Efa - Corp Market). Someone sets how much of each item the market should hold. The page shows what is missing and what is running low, so the industry pilots know what to build. "On shelf" is read from the sell orders listed at the structure; the source is a picker (structure market, my assets, or a corp hangar). Statuses are always a word as well as a colour.
