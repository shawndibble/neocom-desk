## Calculations worth borrowing

- Fee set: broker, sales tax, SCC surcharge, contract tax (site). We have these.
- Per-item "buy order vs instant buy" on shopping list from live spread; we show spread/instant net in Appraisal.
- Auto margin switching: build a sub-component only when profit over threshold.

## Candidate gaps

- Persistent shopping list: add items/fits over days, optional "subtract what I own at X", hauler-fit bar, multibuy copy. Confidence: confirmed in thread (post 3: lowsec player visits Jita weekly); also Omnitool lists "shopping list". Ours: Appraisal is one-shot paste.
- Completed-job history with profit (also Console). Confidence: confirmed; our L13/L81 notes admit the gap.

## Pitfalls

- Site 404 at launch (post 6); no mobile support at launch (posts 1, 4) caused feedback; AI-built disclosure drew caution. Little feedback overall (dev bumped 5 times).

## Out of scope

Invention (decision); nothing else.
