## Calculations worth borrowing

- Freight cost per m3/jump deducted from trade profit (also in EVE Courier arbitrage? no, Courier uses Accounting only).
- Invention EV: cost per attempt / (base chance x (1 + skill terms) x decryptor mult), runs + ME/TE of output, T2 cost incl. datacores. Standard formula; needs SDE industry activity probabilities (already in SDE we ingest for blueprints? verify).
- Compressed-vs-raw: yield * minerals value vs compressed price; our `reprocessing.ts` already has `eff` formula.

## Candidate gaps

1. Invention in Build Plans (decryptor picker, EV cost, T2 chain). Backed by Motor Market, Ravworks (competitors.md 2.3). CONTEXT.md says model "shaped so invention bolts on later". Feasible browser-only: ESI skills + SDE.
2. Cross-plan stock reservation (plan A claims 500 Tritanium so plan B shows shortfall). Motor Market headline feature ("never buy twice").
3. Refine-vs-buy-compressed view for build plans/mining (reuse reprocessing.ts).

## Pitfalls (release notes)

- Duplicate job counting in assignment; "buy" vs "covered from stock" ambiguity; plan load 100s -> instant. Be exact about stock vs buy labels.
- Windows-only .exe; no web. We already win on platform.

## Out of scope

Discord community, desktop packaging.
