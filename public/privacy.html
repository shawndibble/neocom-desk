Privacy Policy — Neocom Desk

Last updated: September 22, 2026

Neocom Desk is an independent, unofficial companion tool for the game EVE Online, built and maintained by Shawn Dibble. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by CCP Games.

What the app reads

You sign in with your EVE Online account through CCP's own official login (EVE SSO) — Neocom Desk never sees or stores your EVE account password. Once signed in, the app reads game data tied to the character(s) you authorize: things like skills, assets, wallet transactions, industry jobs, market orders, and — only if you hold a corporation role and opt in — corporation-level data such as corp wallets or member rosters.

This access is read-only, with one narrow exception: if you mark an in-game mail as read from inside the app, that status is written back to your EVE account.

Where your data lives

Most of what the app reads is cached locally in your browser, on your own device. Your EVE login session (refresh token) never leaves your device — it is not sent to any server the app operator controls, and never appears in logs.

If you turn on cross-device sync, a copy of your plans, settings and similar data is stored on Google Firebase, linked to an anonymous ID derived from your EVE character — not your name, email, or any real-world identity. Removing a character from the app deletes that character's synced data from Firebase.

Crash and error reporting

The app sends crash and error reports to Sentry to help fix bugs. Authorization codes, tokens, and similar secrets are automatically stripped before anything is sent.

Analytics

The app uses Firebase Analytics (Google Analytics) to log which pages you visit, so usage can be understood in aggregate. This is not tied to your name, email, or EVE character — only to an anonymous analytics identifier.

What is never done with your data

  • No data is sold.
  • No data is shared with advertisers.
  • There are no ads in the app.

Third parties involved

  • CCP Games — source of all EVE Online game data, via their public ESI API and SSO login.
  • Google Firebase — optional cross-device sync storage, analytics, and crash reporting infrastructure.
  • Sentry — crash and error reporting.

Your choices

You can stop using sync at any time and clear your local browser data to remove everything the app has cached on your device. To have synced data for a specific character deleted, remove that character from the app, or open an issue at github.com/shawndibble/neocom-desk/issues.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/shawndibble/neocom-desk/issues.
