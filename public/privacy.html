Privacy Policy — Neocom Desk

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Neocom Desk is an independent, unofficial companion tool for the game EVE Online, built and maintained by Shawn Dibble. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by CCP Games.

What the app reads

You sign in with your EVE Online account through CCP's own official login (EVE SSO) — Neocom Desk never sees or stores your EVE account password. Once signed in, the app reads game data tied to the character(s) you authorize: things like skills, assets, wallet transactions, industry jobs, market orders, in-game mail, and — only if you hold a corporation role and opt in — corporation-level data such as corp wallets or member rosters.

Most of this access is read-only. Five actions in the app write back to your EVE account on your behalf, only when you take them: marking a mail as read, sending a mail you composed in the app (including a Community invite or Pledge mail, below), responding (accept/decline/tentative) to a calendar event invite, saving a fitting to your in-game fittings, and setting your autopilot waypoints. The login screen's “Log in with custom permissions” lets you grant less, and EVE's own consent screen lists every permission before you approve it.

Where your data lives

Most of what the app reads is cached locally on your own device. Your EVE login session (refresh token) stays on your device — it is not sent to any server the app operator controls, and never appears in logs.

There is one opt-in exception. If you turn on Alerts when closed for a character in Settings → Notifications, you authorize that character a second time on EVE's login page, for one permission only: reading its notifications. An encrypted copy of that separate notifications-only token is stored on the app operator's server (Google Firebase), so the server can check for new notifications while the app is closed. It cannot read assets, wallet, mail, skills or anything else, and it is never written to your device or to logs. Turn the setting off, remove the character, log out or use Delete all data and the token is deleted and revoked with EVE. Each character is separate: nothing is stored for a character you did not turn it on for.

If you turn on cross-device sync, a copy of your plans, settings and similar data is stored on Google Firebase, linked to an anonymous ID derived from your EVE character — not your name, email, or any real-world identity. Removing a character from the app only clears it from that device. Its synced data is deleted from Firebase automatically once no device has synced that character for 90 days, or right away with Delete all data in the app's Settings. Community data, below, is separate.

Communities (optional)

Communities are the other exception to “what the app reads stays on your device”, and they exist only if you create or join one. Each character joins on its own, by invite link or invite mail, and nothing is shared until you pick it. A Community is stored on Google Firebase, and every Member of that Community (a Member is one character, not a person) can read what is in it. That is:

  • your character's name and EVE character ID, and which Communities it is in. The Community's admin also sees characters waiting to join and the mail invites they sent;
  • the blueprints you choose to share: type, ME/TE, runs and the station or structure and system each is in (read from your assets on your device; never containers, hangars or item IDs);
  • the Fittings you share (name, hull and share code) and Skill Plans you share (name and skill targets);
  • the Requests you post (items, quantities, location, a short note, how you want to hear about Pledges, and your character's name);
  • the Pledges you make: quantities, what you marked delivered, and the IDs of the contracts behind it, taken from your finished contracts on your device.

Invite and Pledge mails are sent from your own character through EVE, so their text goes to EVE and not to the app's server, which only records who was mailed and when to enforce a daily limit. Everything you shared is deleted right away when you leave, are removed, or the Community is deleted. Requests expire 30 days after they are posted or last extended, and are deleted, with their Pledges, 7 days after they expire or close. If no device has synced a character for 90 days it is removed from its Communities, and a Community it administers is deleted. Delete all data does not remove you from a Community: leave it first.

Crash and error reporting

The app sends crash and error reports to Sentry to help fix bugs. Authorization codes, tokens, and similar secrets are automatically stripped before anything is sent.

Analytics

The app uses Firebase Analytics (Google Analytics) to log which pages you visit, so usage can be understood in aggregate. This is not tied to your name, email, or EVE character — only to an anonymous analytics identifier.

What is never done with your data

  • No data is sold.
  • No data is shared with advertisers.
  • There are no ads in the app.

Third parties involved

  • CCP Games — source of all EVE Online game data, via their public ESI API and SSO login.
  • Google Firebase — optional cross-device sync storage, analytics, and crash reporting infrastructure.
  • Sentry — crash and error reporting.

Your choices

You can stop using sync at any time and clear the app's local data on this device to remove everything it has cached. See how to delete your data for the exact steps and what gets removed.
