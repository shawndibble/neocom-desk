Privacy Policy — Neocom Desk

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Neocom Desk is an independent, unofficial companion tool for the game EVE Online, built and maintained by Shawn Dibble. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by CCP Games.

What the app reads

You log in with your EVE Online account through CCP's own official login (EVE SSO) — Neocom Desk never sees or stores your EVE account password. Once logged in, the app reads game data tied to the character(s) you authorize: things like skills, assets, wallet transactions, industry jobs, market orders, location and ship, clones, contacts, standings, in-game mail and notifications, and — only if you hold a corporation role and opt in — corporation-level data such as corp wallets or member rosters.

Most of this access is read-only. Six actions in the app write back to your EVE account on your behalf, only when you take them: marking a mail as read, sending a mail you composed in the app (including a Community invite or Pledge mail, below), responding (accept/decline/tentative) to a calendar event invite, saving a fitting to your in-game fittings, setting your autopilot waypoints, and opening a window (such as an item, contract or market window) in your game client. The login screen's “Log in with custom permissions” lets you grant less, and EVE's own consent screen lists every permission before you approve it.

Where your data lives

Most of what the app reads is cached locally on your own device, in your browser's storage (IndexedDB, local storage and the installed app's cache). Neocom Desk has no account or password of its own and sets no cookies of its own. Your EVE login session (refresh token) stays on your device — it is not sent to any server the app operator controls, and never appears in logs. Your browser talks to CCP's login and ESI servers directly.

There is one opt-in exception. If you turn on Alerts when closed for a character in Settings → Notifications, you authorize that character a second time on EVE's login page, for one permission only: reading its notifications. An encrypted copy of that separate notifications-only token is stored on the app operator's server (Google Firebase), so the server can check for new notifications while the app is closed. It cannot read assets, wallet, mail, skills or anything else, and it is never written to your device or to logs. About every 15 minutes the server uses it to read that character's notifications from EVE and pushes the types you enabled to your device through Google's push service; each push carries the notification's type, time and (size-capped) text. The server keeps only bookkeeping for this — which notifications it has already handled and which notification types it has seen for the character — not the notifications themselves. Turn the setting off, remove the character, log out or use Delete all data and the token is deleted and revoked with EVE. Each character is separate: nothing is stored for a character you did not turn it on for.

There is a second opt-in exception for corporation officers. If a CEO, Director or Accountant turns on Automatic sync in a corporation's Corp Tax Book settings, they authorize their character a second time on EVE's login page, for a narrow set of permissions: the corporation's mining ledger, the corporation wallet, the character's notifications and corporation roles, and structure locations. An encrypted copy of that separate token is stored on the app operator's server (Google Firebase), one per corporation, and about every 6 hours the server uses it to update the corporation's Corp Tax Book (what each character mined, donations paid into the corporation wallet, and which Statements those payments settle). It cannot read mail, assets or skills, send mail or move ISK, and it never issues a Statement or sends a mail. It is never written to your device or to logs. The token is deleted and revoked with EVE when the officer or the corporation's CEO turns Automatic sync off, when the officer loses the role or leaves the corporation, or after 90 days without a sync.

Push notifications are the other opt-in exception. If you turn them on, the app's server (Google Firebase) stores this device's push token, the IDs of the characters on it, and the text of the next 72 hours of alerts, so they can arrive while the app is closed. Alerts already sent are kept for 30 days. To register a character the server checks the EVE access token once to confirm the character is yours; that token is discarded right after and is not stored. When the device's token is dropped (for example when you log out or remove the character), the server deletes its registration and any unsent alert text the next time it runs. If you set a quiet time, its start and end times and your time zone are stored with the device's registration too.

Cross-device sync runs automatically for every character you log in with; there is no separate account and, for now, no switch in Settings to turn it off. To sync, the app sends one short-lived EVE access token to the app operator's server (a Google Firebase function), which verifies it against CCP's published signing keys, does not keep it, and returns a Firebase login for an anonymous ID derived from your EVE character ID — not your name, email, or any real-world identity. Under that ID, a copy of what you make in the app is stored on Google Firebase: Skill Plans, Build Plans, Production Runs, your Market Quickbar, pinned stations, PI picks, Moon Mining payees and assignments, saved Fittings, your daily net worth history, alerts you have already seen, and a list of your synced settings and preferences. What the app reads from EVE (assets, wallet, mail and so on) is not part of this. Removing a character from the app only clears it from that device. Its synced data is deleted from Firebase automatically once no device has synced that character for 90 days, or right away with Delete all data in the app's Settings. When you delete a synced record, a deletion marker stays for 30 days so your other devices catch up, then goes too. Community data, below, is separate unless you choose to leave your Communities in the same step.

If you export a backup (Settings → Data & storage), the app writes a password-encrypted file to wherever you save it. It contains your characters, their login tokens, your synced data and settings. It is created only when you press the button and is never sent to the app operator.

Communities (optional)

Communities are the other exception to “what the app reads stays on your device”, and they exist only if you create or join one. Each character joins on its own, by invite link or invite mail, and nothing is shared until you pick it. A Community is stored on Google Firebase, and every Member of that Community (a Member is one character, not a person) can read what is in it. That is:

  • your character's name and EVE character ID, and which Communities it is in. The Community's admin also sees characters waiting to join and the mail invites they sent;
  • the blueprints you choose to share: type, ME/TE, runs and the station or structure and system each is in (read from your assets on your device; never containers, hangars or item IDs);
  • the Fittings you share (name, hull and share code) and Skill Plans you share (name and skill targets);
  • the Requests you post (items, quantities, location, a short note, how you want to hear about Pledges, and your character's name);
  • the Pledges you make: quantities, what you marked delivered, and the IDs of the contracts behind it, taken from your finished contracts on your device.

Invite and Pledge mails are sent from your own character through EVE, so their text goes to EVE and not to the app's server, which only records who was mailed and when to enforce a daily limit. Everything you shared is deleted right away when you leave, are removed, or the Community is deleted. Requests expire 30 days after they are posted or last extended, and are deleted, with their Pledges, 7 days after they expire or close. If no device has synced a character for 90 days it is removed from its Communities, and a Community it administers is deleted. Delete all data can leave your Communities in the same step (a checkbox, on by default, shown when a character belongs to one), which deletes everything they shared there right away. A Community you administer can't be left, so it stays until you delete it or hand it to another Member.

SRP and the Corp Tax Book (corporation records)

If your corporation uses SRP or a Corp Tax Book, the app keeps that corporation's records on Google Firebase under the corporation's ID. They belong to the corporation, not to one character, so Delete all data does not remove them.

  • SRP Claims: for each claim, the killmail, the claiming character's name and EVE character ID, the hull, solar system and loss value, the fleet or FC and note the pilot gave, the Reimbursement and the claim's state (Pending, Approved, Paid or Rejected). Also the corporation's SRP settings, its Doctrine Fits and its SRP log: each filing (with the pilot's name, killmail, hull and amount) and each officer action (with the officer's name). The corporation's CEO, Directors and Accountants can read all of it; a pilot can read only their own claims.
  • Corp Tax Book: what each character mined at the refineries the book covers (corp members, Guests who mined there from another corporation, and miners at a moon owner's refineries the book collects tax for), the Statements sent, the payments that settle them, write-offs and corrections, a log of which officer did each, and which books bill each character (kept under that character's ID rather than the corporation's). The CEO, Directors, Accountants and Junior Accountants can read the whole book; a corp member can read only their own lines; a moon owner can read only its own mined volume, rate, amount due and payouts, never what each miner owes. A Statement's verify link, sent by in-game mail, shows that Statement to whoever has the link, without logging in.

A Paid or Rejected SRP Claim is deleted automatically 365 days after it closes (the clean-up runs daily). A Pending or Approved claim, the SRP settings and the SRP log have no expiry, so a deleted claim's filing stays in the log; Doctrine Fits stay until a Director or the CEO deletes them. The Corp Tax Book has no expiry either: its lines, Statements, payments, verify links and log stay until the officer who turned the book on, or the corporation's CEO, deletes the book, which deletes everything in it. The record of which books bill a character is kept apart and has no expiry.

Share Links

The app can turn an appraisal, a Fitting, a D-Scan or a mining Survey into a short Share Link. The content (for example the pasted items and quantities, the fitting code, the scan text, a Survey's scans, location and notes, and the character name of the pilot who started a Survey or pasted a scan) is stored on Google Firebase under a random link ID, with no account or character ID attached. Anyone who has the link can read it without logging in, and anyone with a Survey link can add scans to it. A Share Link stops working after 7 days and is then deleted automatically by Firebase, which can take up to a day longer. The app has no way to delete one sooner.

Crash and error reporting

The app sends crash and error reports to Sentry to help fix bugs. A report includes the error, the address of the page it happened on, the addresses of recent requests (which can contain EVE character or item IDs), and browser and device details. The app is set not to attach your IP address, and request and response bodies are not captured. A sample of page loads (about one in five) also sends performance timings. Reports are tagged only as a new or returning visitor, using a timestamp kept on your device. Authorization codes, tokens, and similar secrets are automatically stripped before anything is sent.

Analytics

The app uses Firebase Analytics (Google Analytics) to log which pages you visit, so usage can be understood in aggregate. What is sent is the page's name (for example /market/orders, never a character, plan, share or corporation ID, and never a search or other query text), plus the technical details Google's analytics library collects on its own, such as browser and device type and an anonymous identifier kept in your browser. This is not tied to your name, email, or EVE character. You can turn it off in Settings › Data & storage › Analytics (on by default). When it is off, Google Analytics is never loaded and nothing is sent; the choice stays on this device. Blocking analytics in your browser also works.

What is never done with your data

  • No data is sold.
  • No data is shared with advertisers.
  • There are no ads in the app.

Third parties involved

Your browser contacts these directly, so each sees your IP address along with the request itself:

  • CCP Games — source of all EVE Online game data: the ESI API (esi.evetech.net, which also sees your access token and the character IDs you read), the EVE SSO login (login.eveonline.com) and the Image Server (images.evetech.net, for portraits and item icons).
  • Google (Firebase) — sync and Community storage, SRP Claims and Corp Tax Books, Share Links, login for sync, our server functions, push delivery and Analytics.
  • Sentry — crash and error reporting.
  • GitHub — hosts this site and the app, so it sees ordinary web-server requests for it.
  • Cloudflare — serves the public contract snapshots the app downloads (data.neocomdesk.com); nothing about you is in them.
  • Community EVE services, only when you use the page that needs them: zKillboard (kill history, Threat reads and Route Safety send the IDs of the pilots, systems, regions or hulls you look at), Fuzzwork (market prices: a station or region and item type IDs), EVE-Scout (Thera and Turnur connections; no IDs) and EVE Workbench (the fit ID when you load a Workbench fit link).

The app operator's server also fetches public market, contract and fit data from ESI, EVE Ref and EVE Workbench; none of that involves you. The server's function logs, kept by Google Cloud, can contain character IDs and error messages when something fails, never tokens.

Where it is stored. What the app keeps on Google Firebase (synced data, Communities, SRP Claims, Corp Tax Books, Share Links and push registrations) is stored in the United States, in Firestore's nam5 multi-region, and the server functions run in the United States too. Crash reports go to Sentry's US region, and the public contract snapshots on Cloudflare are stored in Western North America. If you use the app from elsewhere, that data is sent to and stored in the United States. Google Analytics processes its data wherever Google does.

How long it is kept

  • On your device: until you remove the character, log out or delete everything.
  • Synced data: until you delete it, or until no device has synced that character for 90 days; deletion markers 30 days.
  • Alerts you have seen: 30 days, at most 100. Push alert text: the next 72 hours, plus 30 days after sending.
  • Community Requests and Pledges: 30 days after posting or your last extension, then 7 more days; a closed Request 7 days after it closes. Everything else you shared stays until you leave, are removed, or the Community is deleted.
  • Share Links: 7 days.
  • SRP Claims: a Paid or Rejected claim 365 days after it closes. Pending and Approved claims, SRP settings and the SRP log: no expiry. Doctrine Fits: until a Director or the CEO deletes them.
  • Corp Tax Book: no expiry, until the officer who turned it on or the corporation's CEO deletes the book. Which books bill a character: no expiry.
  • Alerts when closed token: until you turn it off, remove the character, log out or delete everything, or the 90-day idle clean-up above.
  • Corp Tax Book Automatic sync token: until the officer or the corporation's CEO turns it off, the officer loses the role or leaves the corporation, or the 90-day idle clean-up above.

Your choices

You can see what the app holds in Settings → Data & storage and in the in-app FAQ (Help), clear the app's local data on this device to remove everything it has cached, and delete your synced data from the app's Settings at any time. See how to delete your data for the exact steps and what gets removed.
