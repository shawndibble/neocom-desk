Delete Your Data — Neocom Desk

Last updated: September 22, 2026

Neocom Desk has no separate account or password — you sign in with your EVE Online character through CCP's own login. Removing a character from the app is how you delete its data, both an account deletion and a data deletion at once.

How to delete a character's data

  1. Open Neocom Desk and sign in.
  2. Go to the Characters page.
  3. Find the character you want removed and click Remove {name} next to it.
  4. Confirm the removal.

To remove every character (delete everything), repeat this for each one.

What gets deleted, and when

  • On your device, immediately: that character's skill plans, build plans, quickbar, and cached game data are deleted from local storage right away.
  • On the sync server, immediately if possible: if a session can still be started as that character, its synced plans, build plans, quickbar, and settings are deleted from Firebase in the same action.
  • On the sync server, deferred: if a session can't be re-established (for example, the character's login is already expired), the synced data is deleted automatically the next time you sign in as that character. There is no other retention period beyond that.

Removing everything without opening the app

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its site data in your browser, immediately deletes all locally cached data for every character. It does not delete synced data left on the server — use the in-app removal above for that, or contact us below.

Need it done for you

If a character's login can no longer be used to sign in and you want its synced data removed manually, open an issue at github.com/shawndibble/neocom-desk/issues naming the character.
