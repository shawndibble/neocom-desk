Delete Your Data — Neocom Desk

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Neocom Desk has no separate account or password — you log in with your EVE Online character through CCP's own login. Your plans and settings sync to the server automatically while you are logged in, and that synced data is deleted in one of two ways: right away with Delete all data, or automatically after 90 days without a sync.

Delete everything now

  1. Open Neocom Desk and log in.
  2. Go to Settings → Data & storage.
  3. Under Delete all data, click Delete all and confirm.

This deletes the synced data of every character logged in on this device from the sync server, along with any Alerts when closed token the server holds for them and this device's push registration, then erases everything the app stores on this device. A character whose login has expired, or a deletion made while offline, cannot reach the server; that character's synced data is then deleted automatically after 90 days without a sync. SRP Claims and Corp Tax Book lines are the corporation's records and stay with it (below).

Remove one character from a device

  1. Open Neocom Desk and log in.
  2. Go to the Characters page.
  3. Find the character you want removed and click Remove {name} next to it.
  4. Confirm the removal.

If the character shared anything with a Community, the confirmation offers to remove its shared blueprints, Fittings and Skill Plans too. Removing a character does not take it out of a Community or delete its Requests and Pledges; leave the Community for that (below).

What gets deleted, and when

  • Alerts when closed, immediately: if you turned this on for a character, the server holds an encrypted, notifications-only token for it. Turning the setting off, removing the character, logging out, or Delete all data deletes that token from the server and revokes it with EVE. If the app cannot reach the server at that moment, the token is deleted automatically after 90 days without a sync, and you can revoke it at any time in your EVE account's authorized applications.
  • Corp Tax Book Automatic sync: if you turned it on for a corporation's book, the server holds an encrypted, narrow-scope token for your character. Turning it off in the book's settings (or the corporation's CEO revoking it), or your character losing the role or leaving the corporation, deletes that token from the server and revokes it with EVE. Delete all data does not reach it; it is deleted automatically after 90 days without a sync, and you can revoke it at any time in your EVE account's authorized applications.
  • Push notifications: if you turned them on, the server holds this device's push token, its character IDs, its quiet-time window and time zone (if set) and the text of the next 72 hours of alerts, plus alerts already sent for 30 days. Logging out or removing the character drops the device's token; the server then deletes its registration and unsent alert text the next time it runs. Sent-alert records are removed after 30 days.
  • On your device, immediately: that character's skill plans, build plans, quickbar, and cached game data are deleted from local storage right away.
  • On the sync server, after 90 days: that character's synced plans, build plans, quickbar, and settings stay on Firebase so that adding the character back restores them. They are deleted automatically once no device has synced that character for 90 days. A record you delete leaves a deletion marker for 30 days so your other devices catch up, then the marker goes too.
  • Share Links, after 7 days: a Share Link's content is not tied to your character and cannot be deleted from the app. It stops working after 7 days and is then deleted automatically (this can take up to a day longer).

Communities

If a character joined a Community, the Delete all data confirmation offers Also leave my Communities, ticked by default. Leaving deletes what the character shared there, including its Requests and Pledges, right away. Untick it and the Community keeps that data until you open Communities, pick the Community and choose Leave. A Community's admin can't leave, so a Community you run is skipped: hand it to another Member or delete it (which deletes it for every Member). Otherwise a character no device has synced for 90 days is removed from its Communities automatically, and a Community it administers is deleted.

SRP Claims and the Corp Tax Book

These belong to the corporation, not to your character, so Delete all data does not remove them, and neither does removing the character, leaving the corporation or the 90-day idle clean-up. They go away like this:

  • SRP Claims: a Paid or Rejected claim is deleted automatically 365 days after it closes. A Pending or Approved claim stays until an officer pays or rejects it, and then follows the same 365 days. The claim's entries in the corporation's SRP log (your filing, with your character's name, and each officer action on it) are deleted with the claim. The SRP settings and the log's other entries have no expiry; Doctrine Fits stay until a Director or the CEO deletes them. The corporation's CEO, Directors and Accountants can read every claim; a pilot can read only their own.
  • Corp Tax Book: what you mined, your Statements, payments, write-offs and verify links have no expiry. They are deleted only when the officer who turned the book on, or the corporation's CEO, deletes the whole book, which also removes the book from the record of which books bill your character. The CEO, Directors, Accountants and Junior Accountants can read the whole book; you can read only your own lines.

See the Privacy Policy for everything each record holds and who else can read it.

Removing everything without opening the app

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its site data on your device, immediately deletes all locally cached data for every character. Synced data left on the server, and any Alerts when closed token, is deleted after 90 days without a sync. To delete it sooner, use Delete all data above, and revoke the token yourself in your EVE account's authorized applications.

See also the Privacy Policy.
