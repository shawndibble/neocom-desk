Delete Your Data — Neocom Desk

Last updated: September 26, 2026

Neocom Desk has no separate account or password — you sign in with your EVE Online character through CCP's own login. Synced data on the server is deleted in one of two ways: right away with Delete all data, or automatically after 90 days without a sync.

Delete everything now

  1. Open Neocom Desk and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Data & storage.
  3. Under Delete all data, click Delete all and confirm.

This deletes the synced data of every character logged in on this device from the sync server, then erases everything the app stores on this device. A character whose login has expired, or a deletion made while offline, cannot reach the server; that character's synced data is then deleted automatically after 90 days without a sync.

Remove one character from a device

  1. Open Neocom Desk and sign in.
  2. Go to the Characters page.
  3. Find the character you want removed and click Remove {name} next to it.
  4. Confirm the removal.

What gets deleted, and when

  • On your device, immediately: that character's skill plans, build plans, quickbar, and cached game data are deleted from local storage right away.
  • On the sync server, after 90 days: if you use sync, that character's synced plans, build plans, quickbar, and settings stay on Firebase so that adding the character back restores them. They are deleted automatically once no device has synced that character for 90 days. There is no other retention period beyond that.

Removing everything without opening the app

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its site data on your device, immediately deletes all locally cached data for every character. Synced data left on the server is deleted after 90 days without a sync. To delete it sooner, use Delete all data above.
