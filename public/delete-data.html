Delete Your Data — Neocom Desk

Last updated: September 26, 2026

Neocom Desk has no separate account or password — you sign in with your EVE Online character through CCP's own login. Removing a character from the app deletes its data from your device; its synced copy on the server is deleted automatically after 90 days without a sync.

How to delete a character's data

  1. Open Neocom Desk and sign in.
  2. Go to the Characters page.
  3. Find the character you want removed and click Remove {name} next to it.
  4. Confirm the removal.

To remove every character (delete everything), repeat this for each one.

What gets deleted, and when

  • On your device, immediately: that character's skill plans, build plans, quickbar, and cached game data are deleted from local storage right away.
  • On the sync server, after 90 days: if you use sync, that character's synced plans, build plans, quickbar, and settings stay on Firebase so that adding the character back restores them. They are deleted automatically once no device has synced that character for 90 days. There is no other retention period beyond that.

Removing everything without opening the app

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its site data in your browser, immediately deletes all locally cached data for every character. Synced data left on the server is deleted after 90 days without a sync — or contact us below to have it removed sooner.

Need it done for you

If you want a character's synced data removed before the 90 days are up, open an issue at github.com/shawndibble/neocom-desk/issues naming the character.
